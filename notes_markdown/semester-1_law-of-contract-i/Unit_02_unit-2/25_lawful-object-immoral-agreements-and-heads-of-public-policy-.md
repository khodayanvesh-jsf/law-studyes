## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 27, an agreement in restraint of trade is:

Explanation. Section 27 voids agreements restraining a lawful trade, profession, or business, with the sole statutory exception being a reasonable restriction on the seller in a sale of goodwill.

In Madhub Chander v. Raj Coomar Doss, the Calcutta High Court held that even a PARTIAL restraint of trade (limited to one locality) is:

Explanation. Indian courts read Section 27 strictly — unlike English law's 'reasonableness' approach, even a partial restraint of trade is void unless it falls within the statutory goodwill exception.

In Superintendence Company of India v. Krishan Murgai, the majority held that a clause barring the employee from competing for two years after he 'left' the company:

Explanation. Tulzapurkar and Untwalia, JJ. read 'leave' as voluntary departure, so the clause never applied to an employee dismissed by the company. They did not decide the Section 27 question. Sen, J., concurring in the result, held that a post-service restraint is void under Section 27.
