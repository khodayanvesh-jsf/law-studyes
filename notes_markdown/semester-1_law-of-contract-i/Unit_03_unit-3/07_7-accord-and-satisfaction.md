# 7. Accord and Satisfaction
**Subject:** Law of Contract – I (Semester 1) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-i/accord-and-satisfaction/

## In One Minute
Accord and satisfaction is a way to end a claim by taking something different from what was promised. The accord is the agreement to accept the substitute. The satisfaction is the giving and accepting of that substitute. When both are complete, the original claim is discharged. The idea sits inside Section 63, which lets a promisee accept "any satisfaction which he thinks fit". In exams it is a short-note topic. In real life it is what a settlement "in full and final satisfaction" of a claim does.

## Legal Provision
- Section 63. Promisee may dispense with or remit performance of promisee. Every promisee may dispense with or remit, wholly or in part, the performance of the promisee made to him, or may extend the time for such performance, or may accept instead of it any satisfaction which he thinks fit. Illustrations. (b) A owes B 5,000 rupees. A pays to B, and B accepts, in satisfaction of the whole debt, 2,000 rupees paid at the time and place at which the 5,000 rupees were payable. The whole debt is discharged. (c) A owes B 5,000 rupees. C pays to B 1,000 rupees, and B accepts them, in satisfaction of his claim on A. This payment is a discharge of the whole claim. (d) A owes B, under a contract, a sum of money, the amount of which has not been ascertained. A, without ascertaining the amount, gives to B, and B, in satisfaction thereof, accepts, the sum of 2,000 rupees. This is a discharge of the whole debt, whatever may be its amount.
- Related provisions
- Section 41. Effect of accepting performance from third person. When a promisee accepts performance of the promise from a third person, he cannot afterwards enforce it against the promisor.
- Section 19. Voidability of agreements without free consent. When consent to an agreement is caused by coercion, fraud or misrepresentation, the agreement is a contract voidable at the option of the party whose consent was so caused.
- Section 19A. Power to set aside contract induced by undue influence. When consent to an agreement is caused by undue influence, the agreement is a contract voidable at the option of the party whose consent was so caused. Any such contract may be set aside either absolutely or, if the party who was entitled to avoid it has received any benefit thereunder, upon such terms and conditions as to the Court may seem just.
- In simple words
- The Act does not use the words " accord and satisfaction ". The idea comes from the words "any satisfaction which he thinks fit" in Section 63. Illustrations (b), (c) and (d) show a claim discharged by a substitute that the promisee accepted.

## Meaning and Definition
Accord and satisfaction means the discharge of a claim by the performance of a substituted obligation that the claimant agreed to accept.

## Key Points to Remember
- The accord is the agreement to accept a substitute. The satisfaction is its performance. The original claim is discharged when the substitute is given and accepted (Section 63). The promisee may accept any satisfaction he thinks fit. A voluntary full and final settlement ends the claim and the right to arbitrate it. A disputed voucher does not by itself bar arbitration; the claimant must prove fraud, coercion, undue influence or economic duress ( Boghara Polyfab ). Consent to a settlement must be free (Sections 14 to 19A). A settlement caused by coercion, fraud or misrepresentation is voidable (Section 19). Novation replaces the contract at once. Accord and satisfaction ends the claim when the substitute is given.
