## Meaning and Definition
What does Section 37 of the Indian Contract Act require of the parties to a contract?

Explanation. Section 37 states that the parties to a contract must perform, or offer to perform, their respective promises, unless such performance is dispensed with or excused under the Act or any other law.

Which of the following is NOT one of the six recognised modes of discharge of a contract?

Explanation. The six modes are performance, agreement, impossibility, lapse of time, operation of law, and breach. Registration is not a recognised mode of discharge.

Novation, rescission, and alteration are all examples of discharge by:

Explanation. Section 62 covers novation, rescission, and alteration together as discharge by mutual agreement between the parties.
