## Meaning and Definition
Specific relief, as opposed to damages, gives the injured party:

Explanation. Specific relief means relief in specie — the actual thing a party was entitled to, not a substitute money value.

The Specific Relief Act, 1963 replaced which earlier statute?

Explanation. The Specific Relief Act, 1963 (Act 47 of 1963) replaced the Specific Relief Act, 1877, and came into force on 1 March 1964.

How many Parts does the Specific Relief Act, 1963 have?

Explanation. The Act has three Parts: Part I (Preliminary), Part II (Specific Relief), and Part III (Preventive Relief), across eight chapters.
