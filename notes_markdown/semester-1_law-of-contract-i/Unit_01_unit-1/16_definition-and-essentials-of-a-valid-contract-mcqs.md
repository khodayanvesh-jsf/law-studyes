## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 10, all agreements are contracts if made by:

Explanation. Section 10 states the test: free consent, competent parties, lawful consideration, lawful object, and not expressly declared void.

Mohori Bibee v. Dharmodas Ghose held that a minor's agreement is:

Explanation. The Privy Council held a minor's agreement is void from the start.

In Raffles v. Wichelhaus, buyer and seller each meant a different ship named 'Peerless'. The case illustrates the need for:

Explanation. Each side meant a different ship, so there was no meeting of minds. It is the classic illustration of consensus ad idem (Section 13).
