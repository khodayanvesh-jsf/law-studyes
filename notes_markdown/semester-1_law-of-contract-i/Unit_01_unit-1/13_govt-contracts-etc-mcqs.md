## Meaning and Definition
Which Article gives the Union and the States the power to make contracts?

Explanation. Article 298 extends executive power to trade, business, property and the making of contracts.

Article 299(1) requires a government contract to be:

Explanation. A contract must be expressed to be made by the President or Governor and executed on their behalf by an authorised person in the authorised manner.

Under Article 299(2), who is personally liable on a government contract?

Explanation. Article 299(2) protects the President, the Governor and the executing person from personal liability.
