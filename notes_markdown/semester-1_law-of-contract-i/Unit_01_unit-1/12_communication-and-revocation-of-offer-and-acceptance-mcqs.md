## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 4, communication of an acceptance is complete as against the proposer when:

Explanation. Communication of acceptance is complete against the proposer the moment it is posted/transmitted — this is the postal rule.

In Felthouse v. Bindley, the court held that:

Explanation. The court held an offeror cannot impose an obligation on the offeree to reject an offer — silence, even when the offeror tries to redefine it as acceptance, is not valid acceptance.

In Lalman Shukla v. Gauri Dutt, the plaintiff's claim for the reward failed because:

Explanation. Lalman had no knowledge of the reward offer at the time he searched for the boy, so there was no valid acceptance — knowledge of the offer is essential.
