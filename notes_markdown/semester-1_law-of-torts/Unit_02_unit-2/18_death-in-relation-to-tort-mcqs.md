## Meaning and Definition
What does the maxim actio personalis moritur cum persona mean?

Explanation. The maxim literally means a personal action dies with the person — at early common law, this extinguished purely personal tort claims on the death of either party.

What was the first aspect of the actio personalis rule, concerning the death of the wrongdoer?

Explanation. At common law, a claim in tort ended when either the wrongdoer or the injured person died, so it could not be continued against the wrongdoer's estate. Section 306 of the Indian Succession Act, 1925 now saves most claims.

In Baker v Bolton (1808), what did Lord Ellenborough hold regarding a claim for the death of the plaintiff's wife?

Explanation. Lord Ellenborough held that the jury could consider only the husband's own bruises, the loss of his wife's society and his distress, from the accident until her death. The damages as to the wife stopped with the period of her existence.
