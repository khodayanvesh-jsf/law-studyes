# 11. Accord and Satisfaction
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/accord-and-satisfaction/

## In One Minute
Accord and satisfaction is a way of ending a claim by agreement. The injured person agrees to accept something in place of what he could claim. That agreement is the accord. What he actually accepts in its place is the satisfaction. Section 63 of the Indian Contract Act says a promisee may accept "any satisfaction which he thinks fit". The illustrations show that a smaller sum, paid and accepted in satisfaction of the whole debt, discharges the whole debt. In exams, this is a short note built on section 63.

## Legal Provision
- Section 63, Indian Contract Act, 1872: every promisee may dispense with or remit , wholly or in part, the performance of the promise made to him. He may extend the time for performance. He may also accept instead of it any satisfaction which he thinks fit. Illustration (b) to section 63: A owes B Rs 5,000. A pays, and B accepts, Rs 2,000 in satisfaction of the whole debt. The Rs 2,000 is paid at the time and place at which the Rs 5,000 were payable. The whole debt is discharged . Illustration (c) to section 63: A owes B Rs 5,000. C pays B Rs 1,000, and B accepts it in satisfaction of his claim on A. The payment discharges the whole claim. Section 62, Indian Contract Act: if the parties to a contract agree to substitute a new contract for it, or to rescind or alter it, the original contract need not be performed. Order XXIII, rule 3, Code of Civil Procedure, 1908: a court records an agreement, compromise or satisfaction that has settled a suit, and passes a decree in those terms. Order XXIV deals with payment into court.
- Section 63 speaks of the promisee and the promise made to him. Order XXIII rule 3 speaks of "a suit", and Order XXIV speaks of a suit "to recover a debt or damages". Order XXIII rule 3 applies to any suit, and Order XXIV applies to a suit for a debt or damages.

## Meaning and Definition
In this note, accord means the agreement by which the injured person consents to accept something different from, or less than, his original claim. Satisfaction means what is actually given and accepted in its place. Section 63 uses the word satisfaction.

Illustrations (b), (c) and (d) to section 63 each describe a sum that is paid and accepted. The discharge in each illustration follows the payment and the acceptance.

## Key Points to Remember
- Accord is the agreement to take a substitute. Satisfaction is what is accepted in its place. Section 63, Contract Act: the promisee may accept any satisfaction he thinks fit. A smaller sum accepted in satisfaction of the whole debt discharges the whole debt (illustration (b)). A third person's payment, accepted in satisfaction, discharges the claim (illustration (c)). Section 135: a composition with, time given to, or a promise not to sue the principal debtor frees the surety unless he assents. Order XXIII rule 3 and Order XXIV apply to suits, including suits for damages. Consent must be free.
