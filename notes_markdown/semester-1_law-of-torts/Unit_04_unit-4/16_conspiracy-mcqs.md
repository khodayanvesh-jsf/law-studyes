## Meaning and Definition
How many persons are required, at minimum, for the tort of conspiracy?

Explanation. Conspiracy, by its very nature, requires a combination of two or more persons — a single individual cannot conspire alone.

What are the essentials of the tort of conspiracy?

Explanation. The essentials are: a combination or agreement between two or more persons, an overt act done in pursuance of it, and actual damage suffered by the plaintiff — conspiracy is not actionable per se.

In a claim for conspiracy to injure by lawful means, what must the plaintiff prove about the defendants' purpose?

Explanation. Lawful means conspiracy requires proof that the combination's predominant purpose was to injure the plaintiff, since each individual act involved is otherwise lawful.
