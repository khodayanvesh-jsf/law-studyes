## Meaning and Definition
Which test, laid down in Sim v Stretch (1936), is now used to determine whether a statement is defamatory?

Explanation. Lord Atkin in Sim v Stretch formulated the modern test: whether the words tend to lower the plaintiff in the estimation of right-thinking members of society generally, replacing the older, narrower 'hatred, ridicule or contempt' test.

What is the essential difference between libel and slander?

Explanation. Libel is defamation in a permanent form (writing, print, pictures), while slander is defamation in a transient form (spoken words or gestures) — the traditional distinction turns on the form, not the content.

A newspaper publishes a false, defamatory report about a shopkeeper, honestly believing it to be true. In a civil claim for defamation:

Explanation. Defamation protects reputation, so an honest belief that the statement is true is not a defence if it is false. Malice matters in other ways: it defeats fair comment and qualified privilege, and it raises damages.
