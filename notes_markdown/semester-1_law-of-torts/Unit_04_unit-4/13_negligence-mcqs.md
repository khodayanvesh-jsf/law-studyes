## Meaning and Definition
What are the three essentials of the tort of negligence?

Explanation. Negligence requires: (1) a duty of care owed by the defendant to the plaintiff, (2) breach of that duty by falling below the standard of care, and (3) damage caused to the plaintiff by that breach.

In Donoghue v Stevenson (1932), Lord Atkin's 'neighbour principle' established that a duty of care is owed to

Explanation. Lord Atkin's neighbour principle extended the duty of care beyond contractual relationships to anyone reasonably foreseeable as likely to be affected by the defendant's acts or omissions.

Why did the manufacturer in Donoghue v Stevenson owe a duty of care to the consumer despite no contract between them?

Explanation. The House of Lords held that a manufacturer owes a duty of care to the ultimate consumer where the product reaches them in the form it left the manufacturer, without reasonable possibility of intermediate examination.
