## Meaning and Definition
What is the primary interest protected by the tort of passing off?

Explanation. Passing off protects a trader's goodwill (built-up reputation) from deceptive misrepresentation by a rival, regardless of whether any trade mark is formally registered.

What are the three elements of the 'classic trinity' test for passing off?

Explanation. The classic trinity for passing off, formulated from Lord Diplock's Erven Warnink test, is: goodwill, misrepresentation, and damage.
