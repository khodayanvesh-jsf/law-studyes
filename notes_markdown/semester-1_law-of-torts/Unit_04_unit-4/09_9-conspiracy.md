# 9. Conspiracy
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 4:** Unit 4
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/conspiracy/

## In One Minute
Civil conspiracy is a combination of two or more persons to injure the claimant, or to use unlawful means against him, that causes him actual damage. One person cannot commit it. It is not actionable without proof of damage. In exams, this topic is asked as "explain the tort of conspiracy with Quinn v. Leathem", and as a problem on trade rivals or a union. The usual trap is to treat any joint competition as conspiracy.

## Legal Provision
- No Indian Act defines civil conspiracy. The tort comes from English common law and is applied in India as justice, equity and good conscience. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, section 61(1): two or more persons who agree to do an illegal act, or a legal act by illegal means, commit criminal conspiracy. proviso to section 61(1): an agreement to commit an offence is complete on the agreement alone. For any other agreement, some act besides the agreement must be done. Section 61(2): conspiracy to commit a serious offence is punished as abetment of that offence, where no express provision exists. Other criminal conspiracy is punished with up to six months, or a fine, or both. Competition Act, 2002, section 3: no enterprise or person shall enter into an agreement on production, supply, distribution, storage, acquisition or control of goods or services that causes or is likely to cause an appreciable adverse effect on competition in India. Such an agreement is void (section 3(2)). Section 3(3): agreements between competitors that fix prices, limit supply, share markets or rig bids are presumed to have an appreciable adverse effect. This includes cartels. Section 27: after inquiry, the Commission can order the agreement to stop and impose a penalty up to ten per cent of the average turnover or income of the last three years. A cartel member can face up to three times its profit for each year, or ten per cent of turnover, whichever is higher. This is a separate statutory regime, not the tort. Trade Unions Act, 1926, section 18(1): no suit lies in a civil court against a registered trade union, or any office-bearer or member, for an act done in contemplation or furtherance of a trade dispute to which a member is a party. The bar applies on the ground only that the act induces another person to break a contract of employment. It also applies where the act interferes with another person's trade, business or employment, or with his right to dispose of his capital or labour. Section 18(2): a registered trade union is not liable in a civil court for a tort done in contemplation or furtherance of a trade dispute by its agent. This applies if it is proved that the agent acted without the knowledge of, or contrary to the express instructions of, the union's executive.

## Meaning and Definition
The tort of conspiracy is committed when two or more persons combine to do an unlawful act, or a lawful act by unlawful means, and actual damage is caused to the claimant. A combination has a power to harm that one person lacks, so the law looks at the combination as a whole.

## Key Points to Remember
- Two or more persons, a common design, an act, actual damage. Lawful means: predominant purpose to injure decides liability. Unlawful means: no predominant purpose needed. Quinn v. Leathem: liable. Mogul Steamship: not liable. Civil conspiracy needs damage. Criminal conspiracy does not.
