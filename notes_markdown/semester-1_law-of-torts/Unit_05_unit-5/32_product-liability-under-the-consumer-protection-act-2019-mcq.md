## Meaning and Definition
Which chapter of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 deals with product liability?

Explanation. Chapter VI (Sections 82 to 87) deals with product liability.

Which section lays down the grounds of liability of a product manufacturer?

Explanation. Section 84 lists the grounds on which a product manufacturer is liable.

Which is NOT a ground of manufacturer liability under Section 84?

Explanation. Section 84 covers manufacturing defect, design defect, deviation from specifications, non-conformity with express warranty and inadequate instructions or warnings.
