## Meaning and Definition
'Unfair trade practice' under Section 2(47) primarily targets

Explanation. Unfair trade practice targets deceptive or unfair methods used to promote sale/use/supply, such as false representations and false guarantees.

Which of the following is expressly listed as an unfair trade practice under the 2019 Act?

Explanation. Section 2(47)(ix) lists disclosing personal information given in confidence by the consumer, unless the disclosure is made in accordance with law.

'Restrictive trade practice' under Section 2(41) primarily targets

Explanation. Restrictive trade practice centres on distorting the market's ordinary functioning (price, delivery, supply), harming consumers collectively.
