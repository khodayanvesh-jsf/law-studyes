Explanation. Section 34 fixes Rs 1 crore unless the Central Government prescribes another value. The 2021 Rules prescribed up to fifty lakh rupees for the District Commission.

Under the 2021 Rules, the State Commission has pecuniary jurisdiction where the consideration paid is

Explanation. The State Commission's pecuniary jurisdiction covers consideration exceeding fifty lakh rupees but not exceeding two crore rupees.
