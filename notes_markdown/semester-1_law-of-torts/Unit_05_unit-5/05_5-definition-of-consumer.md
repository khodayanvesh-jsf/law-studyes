# 5. Definition of Consumer
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/definition-of-consumer/

## In One Minute
A consumer is a person who buys goods or hires services for a consideration. A user of the goods or a beneficiary of the services, with the buyer's approval, is also a consumer. A person who buys goods for resale, or any goods or services for a commercial purpose, is not. In exams, this topic is asked as "define consumer and discuss who is excluded", and as a problem on a business buyer, a free service or a family member. The usual trap is the commercial purpose test and the livelihood exception.

## Legal Provision
- Consumer Protection Act, 2019, section 2(7): defines consumer. Section 2(5): defines complainant. Section 2(42): defines service. It does not include the rendering of any service free of charge or under a contract of personal service. Section 2(16): defines e-commerce. Section 35: who may file a complaint.

## Meaning and Definition
Section 2(7) says that a consumer is any person who does either of two things.

Buys goods. He buys any goods for a consideration which has been paid or promised, or partly paid and partly promised, or under a system of deferred payment. The definition includes any user of the goods, other than the buyer, when the use is with the approval of the buyer. It does not include a person who obtains the goods for resale or for any commercial purpose. Hires or avails of services. He hires or avails of any service for a consideration which has been paid or promised, or partly paid and partly promised, or under a system of deferred payment. The definition includes any beneficiary of the service, other than the hirer, when the service is availed with the approval of the hirer. It does not include a person who avails of a service for any commercial purpose.

## Key Points to Remember
- A consumer buys goods or hires services for a consideration, paid, promised or in instalments. A user or beneficiary with approval is a consumer. Resale and commercial purpose are excluded, except exclusive use for livelihood by self-employment. Free services and personal service contracts are outside the Act. Online and offline buying are covered.
