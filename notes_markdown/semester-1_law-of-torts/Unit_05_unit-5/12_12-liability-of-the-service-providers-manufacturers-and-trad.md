# 12. Liability of the Service Providers, Manufacturers and Traders under the Act- Product Liability
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/liability-of-service-providers-manufacturers-and-traders/

## In One Minute
Product liability is the responsibility of a product manufacturer, a product service provider or a product seller to compensate a consumer for harm caused by a defective product or a deficient service. Chapter VI of the 2019 Act (sections 82 to 87) states who is liable and when. In exams, this topic is asked as "explain product liability under the 2019 Act", and as a problem on a defective product that causes injury. The usual traps are the five grounds against a manufacturer and the point that a manufacturer is liable even without negligence for a breach of express warranty.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(34): "product liability" means the responsibility of a product manufacturer or product seller, of any product or service, to compensate for any harm caused to a consumer by a defective product manufactured or sold, or by deficiency in services relating to it. Section 2(35): "product liability action" is a complaint filed before a District, State or National Commission to claim compensation for the harm. Sections 2(36), 2(37) and 2(38): definitions of product manufacturer, product seller and product service provider. Section 82: the Chapter applies to every claim for compensation under a product liability action for harm caused by a defective product. Section 83: the action lies against a manufacturer, a product service provider or a product seller. Section 84: liability of the product manufacturer. Section 85: liability of the product service provider. Section 86: liability of product sellers. Section 87: exceptions. Section 39(1)(e): the Commission may order compensation in a product liability action.

## Meaning and Definition
Product liability is the responsibility to compensate for harm caused by a defective product, or by faulty service in relation to it. It is a form of liability that rests on the defect and on the part the person played in producing, servicing or selling the product, and not only on carelessness.

## Key Points to Remember
- Chapter VI, sections 82 to 87. Three defendants: manufacturer (section 84), service provider (section 85), seller (section 86). Manufacturer grounds: manufacturing defect, design defect, deviation, breach of express warranty, no adequate instructions or warnings. Manufacturer is liable for the express warranty even without negligence. Exceptions in section 87: misuse, alteration, obvious danger and others.
