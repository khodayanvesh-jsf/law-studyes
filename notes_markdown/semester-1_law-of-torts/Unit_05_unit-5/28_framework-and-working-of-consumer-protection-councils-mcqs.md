## Meaning and Definition
How many tiers of Consumer Protection Councils does the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 establish?

Explanation. The Act establishes a three-tier structure of Consumer Protection Councils — Central, State, and District.

Who chairs the Central Consumer Protection Council?

Explanation. The Central Consumer Protection Council is chaired by the Union Minister in charge of Consumer Affairs.

Who chairs the District Consumer Protection Council?

Explanation. The District Consumer Protection Council is chaired by the District Collector or an equivalent officer.
