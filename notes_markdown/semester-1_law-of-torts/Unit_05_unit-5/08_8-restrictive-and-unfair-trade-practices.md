# 8. Restrictive and Unfair Trade Practices
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/restrictive-and-unfair-trade-practices/

## In One Minute
A restrictive trade practice manipulates prices or the flow of supplies and imposes unjustified costs on consumers. An unfair trade practice uses an unfair or deceptive method to promote sale, use or supply of goods or services. An unfair contract has terms that cause a significant change in the consumer's rights. In exams, this topic is asked as "define unfair trade practice and list its forms", and as a problem on a false claim, a bargain price or a lucky draw. The trap is to mix the three terms and to forget that a bill must be issued.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(41): restrictive trade practice. Section 2(46): unfair contract. Section 2(47): unfair trade practice. Section 2(6)(i): a complaint may allege that an unfair contract or unfair trade practice or restrictive trade practice has been adopted by a trader or service provider. Section 39(1)(g): the Commission may order the opposite party to discontinue the unfair or restrictive trade practice and not to repeat it. Sections 18, 21 and 22: the Central Authority prevents unfair trade practices, and can issue directions, impose penalties and conduct search and seizure. Section 94: measures to prevent unfair trade practices in e-commerce and direct selling.

## Meaning and Definition
A restrictive trade practice is a trade practice that tends to manipulate price or its conditions of delivery, or to affect the flow of supplies in the market relating to goods or services. It does so in a way that imposes unjustified costs or restrictions on consumers.

An unfair trade practice is a trade practice which, to promote the sale, use or supply of goods or the provision of a service, adopts any unfair method or unfair or deceptive practice.

An unfair contract is a contract between a manufacturer, trader or service provider on one side and a consumer on the other, with terms that cause a significant change in the rights of the consumer.

## Key Points to Remember
- Restrictive: manipulating price or supply (section 2(41)). Tie-in sale is an example. Unfair: unfair or deceptive method to promote sales (section 2(47)). Unfair contract: significant change in consumer's rights (section 2(46)). Not issuing a bill and refusing to take back defective goods are unfair trade practices. Remedy: order to discontinue and not repeat, with compensation.
