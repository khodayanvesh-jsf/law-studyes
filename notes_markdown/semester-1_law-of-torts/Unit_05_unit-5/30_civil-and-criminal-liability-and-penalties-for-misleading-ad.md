## Meaning and Definition
What is the fundamental character of civil liability under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019?

Explanation. Civil liability under the Act is compensatory — refund, replacement, compensation, and discontinuation orders — not punitive.

Can civil liability and criminal liability under the Act arise from the same underlying conduct?

Explanation. Civil liability (before a Commission) and criminal liability (before a criminal court) can both arise from the same conduct, serving different purposes through different processes.

Under Section 89, the punishment for a first offence of causing a false or misleading advertisement prejudicial to consumers is

Explanation. Section 89 prescribes imprisonment up to two years and a fine up to ten lakh rupees for a first offence.
