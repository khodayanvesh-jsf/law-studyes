# 13. Remedies
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/remedies/

## In One Minute
If a complaint is proved, the Commission can order the opposite party to remove the defect, replace the goods, refund the price with interest, pay compensation, stop an unfair practice, withdraw hazardous goods, issue a corrective advertisement and pay costs. Section 39 lists these reliefs. In exams, this topic is asked as "what reliefs can a Consumer Commission grant", and as a problem on a defective product with a claim for money. The usual trap is to forget compensation for mental agony and the power to award punitive damages.

## Legal Provision
- Section 39(1): findings and reliefs of the District Commission. Sections 49 and 59 apply the same procedure and reliefs to the State and National Commissions. Section 38(8): interim orders. Section 39(2): sums obtained under section 39(1) are credited to a prescribed fund. Sections 40, 50 and 60: review. Sections 41, 51 and 67: appeals, with a deposit of 50 per cent. Sections 71 and 72: enforcement as a decree and the penalty for non-compliance. Sections 74 to 81: settlement through mediation. Sections 20 and 21: reliefs by the Central Authority: recall of goods, refund and discontinuation.

## Meaning and Definition
A remedy is the relief that the law gives to a person whose right has been violated. Under the Consumer Protection Act, the remedy is the order that a Commission makes when it is satisfied that the goods suffer from a defect, or that the allegations about services, unfair trade practices or product liability are proved.

## Key Points to Remember
- Section 39(1) lists the reliefs: repair, replace, refund with interest, compensation, and more. Compensation includes harassment and mental agony. Punitive damages can be granted in a fit case. Class relief: at least 25 per cent of the value of the goods or services. Non-compliance: imprisonment of one month to three years, or a fine of Rs 25,000 to Rs 1 lakh.
