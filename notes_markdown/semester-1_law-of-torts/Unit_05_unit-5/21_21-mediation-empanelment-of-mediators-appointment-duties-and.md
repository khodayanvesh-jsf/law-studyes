# 21. Mediation: Empanelment of Mediators, appointment, duties and settlement of disputes
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/mediation-under-the-consumer-protection-act/

## In One Minute
Chapter V of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (sections 74 to 81) sets up mediation inside the consumer forums. Each Commission has a consumer mediation cell with a panel of mediators valid for five years. A mediator must disclose any interest, and the Commission records the settlement within seven days. In exams, this topic is asked as a long answer on the empanelment, appointment and duties of mediators and the settlement of disputes. The usual trap is to confuse empanelment, which creates the panel, with nomination, which picks one mediator for one dispute.

## Legal Provision
- Section 37: reference to mediation by the District Commission, with written consent of the parties within five days. Section 74: establishment of the consumer mediation cell. Section 75: empanelment of mediators. Section 76: nomination of mediators from the panel. Section 77: duty of the mediator to disclose certain facts. Section 78: replacement of a mediator. Section 79: procedure for mediation. Section 80: settlement through mediation. Section 81: recording of the settlement and passing of the order. Consumer Protection (Mediation) Rules, 2020: made by the Central Government (G.S.R. 450(E), 15 July 2020, in force from 20 July 2020). They cover the cell, the selection committee, excluded matters, refund of fee and the effect of a settlement. Consumer Protection (Mediation) Regulations, 2020: made by the National Commission (24 July 2020). They cover empanelment, fee, training, code of conduct, procedure, confidentiality and reports.

## Meaning and Definition
Mediation is a way of settling a dispute in which a neutral mediator helps the parties reach an agreement. Empanelment means putting a person on the approved panel of mediators. Nomination means choosing a mediator from that panel for a particular dispute.

## Key Points to Remember
- A mediation cell is attached to every Commission (section 74). The panel is valid for five years, on the recommendation of a selection committee (section 75). The mediator must disclose any interest or doubt about independence (section 77). The mediator is guided by natural justice (section 79). The Commission records the settlement within seven days (section 81). Mediation ends in three months from the first appearance unless extended (Regulation 11(2)). The mediator cannot impose a settlement, and the proceedings are confidential (Regulations 12 and 13). The complainant gets the full application fee back if a settlement is reached (Rule 5).
