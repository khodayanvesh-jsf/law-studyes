# 13. Statutory liability
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/statutory-liability/

## In One Minute
Statutory liability is liability that an Act of Parliament or a State legislature creates. It takes two forms. One is a right to compensation that the Act gives without proof of fault. The other arises when an Act imposes a duty and a person is harmed by its breach. In exams, expect section 164 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, product liability under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the enforcement of statutory duties in Municipal Council, Ratlam v. Vardhichand.

## Legal Provision
- Section 164, Motor Vehicles Act, 1988: the owner or insurer must pay Rs 5 lakh on death and Rs 2.5 lakh on grievous hurt , without proof of fault (section 164(2)). Consumer Protection Act, 2019: gives a remedy for harm caused by a defective product (product liability, sections 82 to 87 in Chapter VI).

## Meaning and Definition
Statutory liability is liability whose source is an Act. The court looks at the words of the Act to decide who is liable, to whom and for how much.

Statutory compensation. The Act itself gives a right to a stated sum or relief. It does not depend on the common law of torts. Breach of statutory duty. The Act puts a duty on a person or body. Whether a person harmed by its breach can claim depends on what the Act provides.

## Key Points to Remember
- Statutory liability is liability created by an Act. Two forms: fixed statutory compensation, and the action for breach of statutory duty. Section 164, Motor Vehicles Act: Rs 5 lakh on death, Rs 2.5 lakh on grievous hurt, no proof of fault. Product liability under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (sections 83 to 87) binds the manufacturer, the service provider and the seller. Municipal Council, Ratlam v. Vardhichand: lack of funds is no defence to a statutory duty. Accepting compensation under section 164 ends the claim petition before the Claims Tribunal (section 166(1), second proviso).
