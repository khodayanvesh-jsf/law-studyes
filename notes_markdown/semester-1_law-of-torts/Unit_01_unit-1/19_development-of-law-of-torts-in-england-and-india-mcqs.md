## Meaning and Definition
The writ of trespass in early English law covered only:

Explanation. The writ of trespass, which took shape in the 13th and 14th centuries, covered direct and forcible harm to the person, land or goods.

"Trespass on the case" developed to cover:

Explanation. Trespass on the case covered indirect harm, where loss followed from the defendant's act. Negligence, nuisance and defamation grew from it.

The Judicature Acts that abolished the rigid forms of action in England were passed in:

Explanation. The Judicature Acts, 1873–1875, merged the courts of law and equity and abolished the old forms of action.
