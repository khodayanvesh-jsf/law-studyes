# 6. Tort distinguished from Crime and Breach of Contract
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester 1) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/tort-distinguished-from-crime-and-breach-of-contract/

## In One Minute
A tort, a crime and a breach of contract are three different wrongs. A tort breaks a duty fixed by law and is remedied by damages. A crime is a public wrong that the State punishes. A breach of contract breaks a duty the parties created by agreement. One act can be more than one wrong. A reckless driver can face a criminal case and a civil suit at once. Examiners ask for the comparison table and for the statement that all torts are civil wrongs but not all civil wrongs are torts.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(m), Limitation Act, 1963: a tort is a civil wrong that is not exclusively a breach of contract or trust. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023: defines the crimes and their punishments. It replaced the Indian Penal Code from 1 July 2024. Sections 73 and 74, Indian Contract Act, 1872: compensation for breach of contract, and liquidated damages or penalty named in the contract. Section 395, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (earlier section 357, Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973): a criminal court may order that fine money be paid as compensation to the victim. Where no fine is imposed, it may order the accused to pay compensation directly (section 395(3)). It does not replace a civil suit.

## Meaning and Definition
A tort is a private civil wrong. A crime is a public wrong made an offence by law. A breach of contract is a failure to perform a promise made by agreement.

All three are wrongs, but the law treats each differently. The difference lies in the source of the duty, in who starts the case, and in what the court does at the end.

Three wrongs and who acts in each How to read this: each box is one kind of wrong. The text says who brings the case and what the court gives. Tort The injured person sues the wrongdoer in a civil court. The court awards damages. Crime The State prosecutes the accused in a criminal court. The court punishes by fine, prison or both. Breach of contract The promisee sues the party who broke the promise. The court awards damages or orders performance. One act can fall in more than one box, for example a drunk driver who injures a pedestrian. A civil suit does not stop a criminal case, and a criminal case does not stop a civil suit.

## Key Points to Remember
- Tort is a private wrong and its main remedy is compensation. A court can also grant an injunction (Specific Relief Act, 1963, sections 36 and 38). Crime is a public wrong and the State prosecutes and punishes. Tort duty is fixed by law and owed to persons generally. Contract duty is fixed by agreement. Proof: balance of probabilities for tort and contract, beyond reasonable doubt for crime. One act can be a tort and a crime, and a civil suit is independent of the criminal case. The same facts can give rise to tort and contract claims (White v. John Warwick & Co.). All torts are civil wrongs, but not all civil wrongs are torts. Compensation for violation of Article 21 is a public law remedy, independent of a tort suit and a prosecution (Nilabati Behera).
