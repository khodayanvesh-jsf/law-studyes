Explanation. The Patna High Court treated the grossly unfair price as strong evidence that the seller could not understand the transaction or judge whether it was in his own interest — the sale was held void for want of capacity.

Explanation. Section 86 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 requires the Central Government's consent before a foreign sovereign, ambassador, or accredited diplomat can be sued in an Indian court.
