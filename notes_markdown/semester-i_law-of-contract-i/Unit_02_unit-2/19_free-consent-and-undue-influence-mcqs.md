## Meaning and Definition
In Chikkam Ammiraju v. Chikkam Seshamma, a threat to commit suicide unless a release deed was executed was held to be:

Explanation. The Madras High Court (1917) held that a threat of suicide amounted to coercion under Section 15, since the Court treated the threatened act as one forbidden by the Indian Penal Code. The release deed was voidable.

Which of the following is a key difference between coercion and undue influence?

Explanation. Undue influence, by its nature, requires a relationship in which one party can dominate the other's will; coercion can come from anyone, including a total stranger to the contract.

On the Raghunath Prasad v. Sarju Prasad approach to Section 16, once a relationship of dominance and an unconscionable transaction are shown, the burden of proof:

Explanation. Raghunath Prasad sets a three-stage approach: dominance, then inducement by undue influence, then the burden of proof. Under Section 16(3), once dominance and an unconscionable bargain are shown, the dominant party must prove the transaction was not induced by undue influence. High interest alone, without proof of dominance, raises no presumption.
