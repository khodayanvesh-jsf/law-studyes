# 12. illegal agreements
**Subject:** Law of Contract – I (Semester I) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-i/illegal-agreements/

## In One Minute
An illegal agreement is one whose object or consideration the law forbids or condemns on a ground in Section 23 (forbidden by law, defeating a law, fraudulent, injurious to another, immoral or opposed to public policy). Every illegal agreement is void , so no one can sue on it. But not every void agreement is illegal . A wager (Section 30) or an agreement without consideration (Section 25) is void but not illegal. The difference matters in three ways: Collateral deals. A connected deal made to further an illegal purpose is also tainted. A connected deal to a merely void agreement is not ( Gherulal Parakh ). Getting money back. Where both parties knowingly took part in an illegal deal, the court usually leaves them where it finds them ( in pari delicto ). Legal and illegal parts. One unlawful part spoils a single bargain (Section 24), but Sections 57 and 58 save the legal part of reciprocal and alternative promises. Exams ask for the void and illegal contrast. In real life it decides whether a lender or an agent can recover money linked to a bad deal.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(g). An agreement not enforceable by law is said to be void.
- Section 23 (last paragraph). In each of these cases, the consideration or object of an agreement is said to be unlawful. Every agreement of which the object or consideration is unlawful is void.
- Section 24. If any part of a single consideration for one or more objects, or any one or any part of any one of several considerations for a single object, is unlawful, the agreement is void.
- Section 57. Reciprocal promise to do things legal, and also other things illegal. Where persons reciprocally promise, firstly, to do certain things which are legal, and, secondly, under specified circumstances, to do certain other things which are illegal, the first set of promises is a contract, but the second is a void agreement. Illustration. A and B agree that A shall sell B a house for 10,000 rupees, but that, if B uses it as a gambling house, he shall pay A 50,000 rupees for it. The first set of reciprocal promises, namely, to sell the house and to pay 10,000 rupees for it, is a contract. The second set is for an unlawful object, namely, that B may use the house as a gambling house, and is a void agreement.
- Section 58. Alternative promise, one branch being illegal. In the case of an alternative promise, one branch of which is legal and the other illegal, the legal branch alone can be enforced. Illustration. A and B agree that A shall pay B 1,000 rupees, for which B shall afterwards deliver to A either rice or smuggled opium. This is a valid contract to deliver rice, and a void agreement as to the opium.
- Section 65. When an agreement is discovered to be void, or when a contract becomes void, any person who has received any advantage under such agreement or contract is bound to restore it, or to make compensation for it to the person from whom he received it. Illustration (a). A pays B 1,000 rupees in consideration of B's promising to marry C, A's daughter. C is dead at the time of the promise. The agreement is void, but B must repay A the 1,000 rupees.
- In simple words
- An agreement with an unlawful object or consideration is void (Section 23). If a single bargain is partly unlawful, the whole is void (Section 24). If the legal and illegal promises are separate sets (Section 57) or alternatives (Section 58), the legal part survives. Money or benefits taken under an agreement later "discovered" to be void must be returned (Section 65), but not where both sides knowingly joined in an illegal deal.

## Meaning and Definition
The Act does not define "illegal". It speaks of an unlawful object or consideration and says such an agreement is void (Section 23). In practice, an illegal agreement is one the law forbids or condemns on a ground in Section 23.

A void agreement is wider. It includes agreements void for reasons short of illegality, such as an agreement without consideration (Section 25), an uncertain agreement (Section 29) or a wager (Section 30).

## Key Points to Remember
- Illegal agreements are void. Not every void agreement is illegal. No action can be founded on an illegal agreement. A collateral deal made knowingly to further an illegal purpose is tainted. A collateral deal to a merely void agreement is not automatically tainted. Section 65: advantages under an agreement discovered to be void must be restored. Where both parties are equally at fault, the court usually leaves them where it finds them. Section 24: one unlawful part of a single bargain spoils the whole. Sections 57 and 58 save the legal part in reciprocal and alternative promises.
