# 10. Lawful Object
**Subject:** Law of Contract – I (Semester I) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-i/lawful-object/

## In One Minute
An agreement is a contract only if its consideration and its object are lawful (Section 10). Section 23 says both are lawful unless one of five grounds applies: it is forbidden by law ; if permitted, it would defeat the provisions of any law ; it is fraudulent ; it involves or implies injury to the person or property of another ; the Court regards it as immoral or opposed to public policy . If any ground applies, the consideration or object is unlawful , and the agreement is void . If even part of a single consideration or object is unlawful, the whole agreement is void (Section 24). Void is wider than unlawful. A wager is void (Section 30) but not forbidden by law ( Gherulal Parakh v. Mahadeodas Maiya ). In exams, the five grounds and the Act's illustrations earn the marks. In real life, no court will enforce a deal to break the law.

## Legal Provision
- Section 10. What agreements are contracts. All agreements are contracts if they are made by the free consent of parties competent to contract, for a lawful consideration and with a lawful object, and are not hereby expressly declared to be void.
- Section 23. What considerations and objects are lawful, and what not. The consideration or object of an agreement is lawful, unless— it is forbidden by law; or is of such a nature that if permitted, it would defeat the provisions of any law; or is fraudulent; or involves or implies injury to the person or property of another; or the Court regards it as immoral, or opposed to public policy. In each of these cases, the consideration or object of an agreement is said to be unlawful. Every agreement of which the object or consideration is unlawful is void.
- Section 24. Agreements void, if considerations and objects unlawful in part. If any part of a single consideration for one or more objects, or any one or any part of any one of several considerations for a single object, is unlawful, the agreement is void. Illustration. A promises to superintend, on behalf of B, a legal manufacture of indigo, and an illegal traffic in other articles. B promises to pay to A a salary of 10,000 rupees a year. The agreement is void, the object of A's promise, and the consideration for B's promise, being in part unlawful.
- Related sections. Section 2(g): "An agreement not enforceable by law is said to be void." Sections 57 and 58 save the legal part of reciprocal and alternative promises (see Illegal agreements ). Section 65: a person who has received an advantage under an agreement discovered to be void must restore it or make compensation.
- In simple words
- Section 10: a lawful consideration and a lawful object are two of the essentials of every contract. Section 23: what you promise, and what you aim at, must not break the law, get round the law, cheat, harm someone, or offend morality or public policy . If they do, the agreement is void. Section 24: if even a part of a single bargain is unlawful, the whole bargain fails.

## Meaning and Definition
Object is the purpose the parties set out to achieve by the agreement.

Consideration is what each party gives or promises in return . Under Section 2(d) it is an act, an abstinence or a promise, done or made at the promisor's desire by the promisee or any other person.

Section 23 treats both alike: if either is unlawful, the agreement is void.

How Section 23 is worded. It starts from lawfulness: the consideration or object "is lawful, unless" a listed ground applies. So the party who says an agreement is unlawful must bring it within one of the grounds.

The five grounds at a glance.

## Key Points to Remember
- Section 23 makes the consideration or object lawful unless a listed ground applies. The grounds: forbidden by law, defeating a law, fraud, injury to another, and immorality or public policy. An agreement with an unlawful object or consideration is void. Section 24: an unlawful part of a single bargain makes the whole agreement void. "Forbidden by law" means acts punishable under the penal law or prohibited by special laws. Void is wider than unlawful: a wager is void but not unlawful. Section 65: an advantage received under an agreement discovered to be void must be restored.
