## Meaning and Definition
X agrees to sell his car to B for ₹50,000 or ₹1,00,000, with nothing in the agreement showing which price was meant. This agreement is:

Explanation. Since the price term has no fixed, ascertainable meaning and nothing in the agreement resolves which figure applies, it is void for uncertainty under Section 29.

In Gherulal Parakh v. Mahadeodas Maiya, the Supreme Court held that a partnership formed to carry on wagering transactions is:

Explanation. The Court held that wagering agreements are void under Section 30 but are not 'immoral' or otherwise unlawful under Section 23 — so a partnership formed to conduct a wagering business is not itself illegal.

A collateral agreement connected to a main agreement that is merely VOID (but not illegal):

Explanation. A collateral deal made to further an illegal purpose is tainted. If the main agreement is merely void, such as a wager, a collateral deal is not automatically tainted (Gherulal Parakh v. Mahadeodas Maiya). This is the key practical difference between void and illegal.
