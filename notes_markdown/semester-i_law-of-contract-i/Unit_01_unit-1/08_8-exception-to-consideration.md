# 8. Exception to consideration
**Subject:** Law of Contract – I (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-i/exception-to-consideration/

## In One Minute
The general rule is "no consideration, no contract" . Section 25 says an agreement made without consideration is void . Section 25 itself makes three exceptions . A promise without consideration is still a contract if it is: in writing, registered, and made out of natural love and affection between near relations (Section 25(1)); a promise to compensate a person who voluntarily did something for the promisor in the past (Section 25(2)); or a written, signed promise to pay a time-barred debt (Section 25(3)). A gift actually made cannot be undone for want of consideration (Explanation 1). No consideration is needed to create an agency (Section 185). Examiners love this list. In real life, it explains when a family promise or a thank-you payment can be enforced.

## Legal Provision
- Section 25. An agreement made without consideration is void, unless— (1) it is expressed in writing and registered under the law for the time being in force for the registration of documents, and is made on account of natural love and affection between parties standing in a near relation to each other; or unless (2) it is a promise to compensate, wholly or in part, a person who has already voluntarily done something for the promisor, or something which the promisor was legally compellable to do; or unless (3) it is a promise, made in writing and signed by the person to be charged therewith, or by his agent generally or specially authorized in that behalf, to pay wholly or in part a debt of which the creditor might have enforced payment but for the law for the limitation of suits. In any of these cases, such an agreement is a contract. Explanation 1. Nothing in this section shall affect the validity, as between the donor and donee, of any gift actually made. Explanation 2. An agreement to which the consent of the promisor is freely given is not void merely because the consideration is inadequate; but the inadequacy of the consideration may be taken into account by the Court in determining the question whether the consent of the promisor was freely given.
- Section 185. No consideration is necessary to create an agency.
- Illustration (a) to Section 25. A promises, for no consideration, to give to B Rs. 1,000. This is a void agreement.
- In simple words
- The rule: a bare promise to give something for nothing is void. Exception 1: a family promise made out of love, if it is written and registered . Exception 2: a promise to pay someone who already helped you on his own . Exception 3: a written and signed promise to pay an old debt that can no longer be sued on. Explanation 1: a gift already handed over stays valid. Section 185: an agent can be appointed without consideration .

## Meaning and Definition
An exception to consideration is a case where the law enforces a promise even though nothing was given in return for it. In each case Section 25 says: "such an agreement is a contract ".

Why the law makes exceptions. The rule "no consideration, no contract" protects people from careless, one-sided promises. But in some cases there is good reason to enforce a promise without consideration.

Love and affection within a family is a real reason. Writing and registration show the promise was serious. Gratitude for a past voluntary service rests on a real benefit already received. An old debt is still a moral duty. The law of limitation takes away only the remedy, not the debt itself.

## Key Points to Remember
- An agreement without consideration is void (Section 25). Section 25(1): written + registered + natural love and affection + near relation. Section 25(2): promise to compensate a past voluntary act done for the promisor, or what he was legally compellable to do. Section 25(3): written and signed promise to pay a time-barred debt , wholly or in part. A mere acknowledgment is not enough. Before the limitation period ends, a signed acknowledgment (Section 18) or an acknowledged payment (Section 19) of the Limitation Act gives a fresh period. Explanation 1: a completed gift stands. Explanation 2: inadequacy alone does not make an agreement void. Section 185: no consideration is needed for agency . Section 63: a promisee may remit performance without consideration. A subscription promise binds only after the promisee acts on it.
