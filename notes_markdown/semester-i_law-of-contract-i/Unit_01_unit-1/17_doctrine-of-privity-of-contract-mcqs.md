## Meaning and Definition
The doctrine of privity of contract means:

Explanation. Privity of contract means only the actual parties to an agreement have standing to sue or be sued on it — a stranger generally cannot enforce it.

In Dunlop Pneumatic Tyre Co. v. Selfridge & Co., Dunlop could not sue Selfridge because:

Explanation. Only a party can sue on a contract, and no consideration moved from Dunlop to Selfridge. All the Lords relied on the lack of consideration, and Lord Haldane also relied on privity.

Which of these is NOT a recognised exception to privity of contract in India?

Explanation. Inadequate consideration is unrelated to privity exceptions. The recognised exceptions include trusts/beneficiaries, family arrangements, agency, assignment, and acknowledgment/estoppel.
