## In One Minute
A standard form contract is a contract whose terms are printed or written in advance by one party (usually a big company, the State or a service provider) and offered to everyone on a "take it or leave it" basis. The other party cannot bargain; he can only sign, click or take the ticket. Railway and bus tickets, insurance policies, bank forms, courier consignment notes, dry-cleaning bills, builder-buyer agreements and website "I agree" terms are all standard forms. In the USA they are called "contracts of adhesion" ( LIC of India v. Consumer Education & Research Centre ). Such contracts save time, but they are often used to slip in exemption clauses that cut down the stronger party's liability. The law protects the weaker party in five ways: Notice. A printed term binds only if reasonable notice of it was given before or at the time of the contract ( Parker ; Olley ; Thornton ). A mere receipt is not a contract document ( Chapelton ). Signature. A person who signs a document is normally bound by it, read or not ( L'Estrange v. Graucob ; Bharathi Knitting v. DHL ). Strict reading. An unclear term is read against the party who drafted it ( contra proferentem ). Unfair terms. Courts will not enforce an unfair and unreasonable clause imposed by a party with superior bargaining power; such a clause can be void under Section 23 as opposed to public policy ( Brojo Nath Ganguly ; Lilly White ). A clause barring the usual legal proceedings is void under Section 28 . Consumer law. Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the State and National Commissions can declare an unfair contract term null and void (Sections 2(46), 49(2), 59(2)); Pioneer v. Govindan Raghavan applied this to a builder's one-sided agreement. Online terms accepted by clicking "I agree" are standard forms too; such contracts are valid under Section 10A of the IT Act, and the same protections apply. In the exam, define, give examples, then explain each protection with a case. In real life, this tells you when the "terms and conditions" on a bill, ticket or website really bind you.

## Legal Provision
- There is no single section on standard form contracts . The rules come from case law and these provisions.
- Section 23, Indian Contract Act, 1872. What considerations and objects are lawful, and what not. The consideration or object of an agreement is lawful, unless— it is forbidden by law; or is of such a nature that if permitted, it would defeat the provisions of any law; or is fraudulent ; or involves or implies injury to the person or property of another; or the Court regards it as immoral, or opposed to public policy. In each of these cases, the consideration or object of an agreement is said to be unlawful. Every agreement of which the object or consideration is unlawful is void.
- Section 28, Indian Contract Act, 1872 (main part). Agreements in restraint of legal proceedings, void. Every agreement,— (a) by which any party thereto is restricted absolutely from enforcing his rights under or in respect of any contract, by the usual legal proceedings in the ordinary tribunals, or which limits the time within which he may thus enforce his rights; or (b) which extinguishes the rights of any party thereto, or discharges any party thereto, from any liability, under or in respect of any contract on the expiry of a specified period so as to restrict any party from enforcing his rights, is void to the extent.
- Section 10A, Information Technology Act, 2000. Validity of contracts formed through electronic means. Where in a contract formation, the communication of proposals, the acceptance of proposals, the revocation of proposals and acceptances, as the case may be, are expressed in electronic form or by means of an electronic records, such contract shall not be deemed to be unenforceable solely on the ground that such electronic form or means was used for that purpose.
- Section 2(46), Consumer Protection Act, 2019. "unfair contract" means a contract between a manufacturer or trader or service provider on one hand, and a consumer on the other, having such terms which cause significant change in the rights of such consumer, including the following, namely:— (i) requiring manifestly excessive security deposits to be given by a consumer for the performance of contractual obligations; or (ii) imposing any penalty on the consumer, for the breach of contract thereof which is wholly disproportionate to the loss occurred due to such breach to the other party to the contract; or (iii) refusing to accept early repayment of debts on payment of applicable penalty; or (iv) entitling a party to the contract to terminate such contract unilaterally, without reasonable cause; or (v) permitting or has the effect of permitting one party to assign the contract to the detriment of the other party who is a consumer, without his consent; or (vi) imposing on the consumer any unreasonable charge, obligation or condition which puts such consumer to disadvantage;
- Section 49(2), Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Without prejudice to the provisions of sub-section (1), the State Commission may also declare any terms of contract, which is unfair to any consumer, to be null and void.
- Section 59(2), Consumer Protection Act, 2019. Without prejudice to sub-section (1), the National Commission may also declare any terms of contract, which is unfair to any consumer to be null and void.
- In simple words
- Section 23: an agreement (or a clause) whose object is opposed to public policy is void . The Supreme Court used this to strike down an unfair clause in a standard form ( Brojo Nath Ganguly ). Section 28: a clause that stops a party from going to court at all, or cuts down the time for suing, is void to that extent . Arbitration clauses are saved by the exceptions to the section. Section 10A, IT Act: a contract is not invalid merely because the offer and acceptance were made electronically , for example by clicking "I agree". Section 2(46), CPA 2019: an unfair contract is a contract between a trader or service provider and a consumer with terms that significantly change the consumer's rights , such as excessive deposits, disproportionate penalties or one-sided termination. Sections 49(2) and 59(2), CPA 2019: the State Commission and the National Commission can declare an unfair term null and void . A complaint that an unfair contract has been adopted is a "complaint" under Section 2(6)(i); such complaints go to the State Commission (consideration up to ₹10 crore, Section 47(1)(a)(ii)) or the National Commission (above ₹10 crore, Section 58(1)(a)(ii)).

## Meaning and Definition
What a standard form contract is

A standard form contract is a contract in which:

the terms are drafted in advance by one party , usually in print or on a screen; the same terms are offered to every customer ; the other party has no real chance to bargain ; he must accept the whole form or go without the goods or service.

The Supreme Court described the position in LIC of India v. Consumer Education & Research Centre (1995): "In dotted line contracts there would be no occasion for a weaker party to bargain or to assume to have equal bargaining power. He has either to accept or leave the services or goods in terms of the dotted line contract."

Other names

Contract of adhesion: "In USA, the standard forms of contracts are called 'Contracts of Adhesion'" ( LIC ). The weaker party merely "adheres" to terms set by the other. Dotted line contract: the customer only signs "on the dotted line" ( Brojo Nath Ganguly ; Pioneer ). E-contracts: online standard forms. In a click-wrap contract the user clicks "I agree" after the terms are shown; in a browse-wrap contract the terms are only on a link and the site says that using it means accepting them; in a shrink-wrap contract the terms come inside the package of a product such as software.

Why they are used, and the danger

Advantages Dangers Saves time when thousands of similar contracts are made (railways, insurance, banks) The customer usually does not read the small print Same terms for everyone; easy to administer The drafter can insert exemption clauses limiting his liability Lower cost of each transaction Unequal bargaining power: "take it or leave it"

On the back, in small print: "Liability for loss or damage is limited to 10 times the cleaning charge." The saree is lost. ## Key Points to Remember
- Standard form: terms drafted by one party; "take it or leave it"; "contracts of adhesion" ( LIC ). Unsigned terms bind only with reasonable notice ( Parker ). Notice must be before or at the time of contract ( Olley ; Thornton ). A mere receipt is not a contract document ( Chapelton ). Signed terms bind, read or not ( L'Estrange ; Bharathi Knitting ). Contra proferentem : unclear terms read against the drafter. Unfair clause between unequal parties is void under Section 23 ( Brojo Nath Ganguly ). Clause barring courts is void under Section 28 ; arbitration saved. CPA 2019: State and National Commissions may declare unfair terms null and void (Sections 49(2), 59(2)); Pioneer . Click-wrap contracts are valid (IT Act, Section 10A).
