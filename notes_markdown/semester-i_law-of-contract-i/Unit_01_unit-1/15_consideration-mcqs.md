## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 2(d), consideration must move at:

Explanation. Consideration must be given at the desire of the promisor — an act done voluntarily or at someone else's request is not valid consideration.

In Durga Prasad v. Baldeo, the plaintiff's claim failed because:

Explanation. His original effort was done at the Collector's request, not the shopkeepers' — so it wasn't valid consideration for their later promise to pay commission.

Which of these is required for consideration to be valid?

Explanation. Consideration must be real and lawful, but need not be adequate — courts don't assess fairness of the exchange, only that something of value was genuinely given.
