Which of the following is NOT one of the four categories of contract excluded under Section 14?

Explanation. A contract for sale of a specific house is normally specifically enforceable; the four exclusions are substituted performance obtained, unsupervisable continuous duty, personal-skill contracts, and determinable contracts.
