## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 20A, a court cannot grant an injunction where doing so would:

Explanation. Section 20A(1) creates an absolute bar on injunctions that would impede or delay a Schedule-listed infrastructure project.

The Schedule listing infrastructure categories was inserted into the Specific Relief Act by:

Explanation. The Schedule, read with Sections 20A and 41(ha), was inserted by the Specific Relief (Amendment) Act, 2018.

Which of the following is NOT one of the Schedule's five infrastructure categories?

Explanation. The five categories are Transport, Energy, Water and Sanitation, Communication, and Social & Commercial Infrastructure — not entertainment/media.
