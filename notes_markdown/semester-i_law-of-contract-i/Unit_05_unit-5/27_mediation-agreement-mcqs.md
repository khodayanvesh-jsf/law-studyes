## Meaning and Definition
Under the Mediation Act, 2023, a mediation agreement must be:

Explanation. Section 4 requires a mediation agreement to be in writing. The note records that sections 4, 5, 6, 27 and 28 come into force on a date the Central Government notifies.

In mediation, who decides the outcome of the dispute?

Explanation. The mediator only helps the parties reach a settlement; the parties decide.

Pre-litigation mediation under Section 5 of the Mediation Act, 2023 is:

Explanation. Section 5 is voluntary, but its proviso keeps Section 12A of the Commercial Courts Act for commercial disputes.
