## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 59, who has the first right to appropriate a payment to a specific debt?

Explanation. Section 59 gives the debtor the first right to direct which debt a payment should be applied to, either expressly or by clear implication.

If the debtor does not specify which debt a payment is for, Section 60 allows the creditor to apply it to:

Explanation. Section 60 gives the creditor discretion to apply an unspecified payment to any lawful debt, including one barred by the Limitation Act.

If neither the debtor nor the creditor appropriates a payment, Section 61 applies it:

Explanation. Section 61 applies unappropriated payments to debts in chronological order — the earliest debt is treated as discharged first.
