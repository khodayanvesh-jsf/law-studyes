## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 38, which of the following is NOT a requirement for a valid tender?

Explanation. A tender is valid even if refused — Section 38 discharges the promisor from liability once a valid tender is proved, regardless of whether the promisee accepts it.

What is the legal effect of a valid tender that the promisee wrongfully refuses to accept?

Explanation. Section 38 provides that a promisor who has made a valid tender is not responsible for non-performance and does not lose any rights under the contract.

Under Section 40, who must perform a contract requiring personal skill, such as painting a portrait?

Explanation. Section 40 requires personal performance where the contract shows the parties intended it, or where the nature of the contract requires personal skill or credit.
