## Meaning and Definition
Section 62 covers all of the following EXCEPT:

Explanation. Section 62 covers novation, rescission, and alteration as forms of discharge by agreement. Frustration is covered separately under Section 56.

What distinguishes rescission from novation?

Explanation. Rescission is a plain cancellation with no replacement, whereas novation substitutes an entirely new contract for the old one.

Under Section 63, a promisee can do all of the following WITHOUT needing fresh consideration EXCEPT:

Explanation. Section 63 allows a promisee to remit, extend time, or accept different satisfaction without fresh consideration — it does not allow demanding more than originally promised.
