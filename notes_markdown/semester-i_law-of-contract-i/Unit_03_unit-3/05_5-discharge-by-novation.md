# 5. Discharge by Novation
**Subject:** Law of Contract – I (Semester I) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-i/discharge-by-novation/

## In One Minute
Novation means the parties replace an existing contract with a new one. The old contract ends at once, and only the new one can be enforced. The new contract may be between the same parties on new terms, or it may bring in a new party in place of an old one. Every party must agree. Without that, the old contract stands. In exams, novation is a short-note topic: give the section, the two forms, the need for consent and the effect. In real life it explains how a debt or a contract is moved from one person to another.

## Legal Provision
- Section 62. Effect of novation, rescission, and alteration of contract. If the parties to a contract agree to substitute a new contract for it, or to rescind or alter it, the original contract, need not be performed. Illustrations. (a) A owes money to B under a contract. It is agreed between A, B and C that B shall thenceforth accept C as his debtor, instead of A. The old debt of A to B is at an end, and a new debt from C to B has been contracted. (b) A owes B 10,000 rupees. A enters into an arrangement with B and gives B a mortgage of his (A's) estate for 5,000 rupees in place of the debt of 10,000 rupees. This is a new contract and extinguishes the old. (c) A owes B 1,000 rupees under a contract. B owes C 1,000 rupees B orders A to credit C with 1,000 rupees in his books, but C does not assent to the arrangement. B still owes C 1,000 rupees, and no new contract has been entered into.
- Related provisions
- Section 135. Discharge of surety when creditor compounds with, gives time to, or agrees not to sue, principal debtor. A contract between the creditor and the principal debtor, by which the creditor makes a composition with, or promises to give time to, or not to sue, the principal debtor, discharges the surety, unless the surety assents to such contract.
- Section 137. Creditor's forbearance to sue does not discharge surety. Mere forbearance on the part of the creditor to sue the principal debtor or to enforce any other remedy against him does not, in the absence of any provision in the guarantee to the contrary, discharge the surety.
- Section 139. Discharge of surety by creditor's act or omission impairing surety's eventual remedy. If the creditor does any act which is inconsistent with the rights of the surety, or omits to do any act which his duty to the surety requires him to do, and the eventual remedy of the surety himself against the principal debtor is thereby impaired, the surety is discharged.
- Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, Section 16(1). The arbitral tribunal may rule on its own jurisdiction, including ruling on any objections with respect to the existence or validity of the arbitration agreement, and for that purpose,— (a) an arbitration clause which forms part of a contract shall be treated as an agreement independent of the other terms of the contract; and (b) a decision by the arbitral tribunal that the contract is null and void shall not entail ipso jure the invalidity of the arbitration clause.
- In simple words
- If the parties agree to put a new contract in place of the old one, the old one need not be performed. The new contract may be between the same parties (Illustration (b)) or with a new party (Illustration (a)). If anyone whose consent is needed does not agree, there is no new contract (Illustration (c)). The same section also covers rescission (cancelling) and alteration (changing terms).

## Meaning and Definition
Novation is the substitution of a new contract for an existing one, by agreement of all the parties. The word comes from the Latin novare , "to make new". Section 62 does not use the word "novation" in its text, though its heading does. The text describes the idea in the words "substitute a new contract".

The two forms of novation

Form What changes Illustration to Section 62 Same parties, new terms A new contract between the same parties replaces the old (b): a mortgage for 5,000 rupees in place of a debt of 10,000 rupees New party A new person replaces an old party, with everyone's consent (a): C replaces A as B's debtor

Why the law allows novation. The parties made the first contract, so they may replace it. Novation gives the old contract a clean end, so no one can later enforce both.

## Key Points to Remember
- Novation is the replacement of a contract by a new contract (Section 62). It may be between the same parties on new terms, or it may substitute a party. Every party must consent, including the new party. The old contract is discharged and cannot be enforced. A conditional or partial substitute is not a novation ( Lata Construction ). Rescission cancels. Alteration changes terms. Novation replaces. A creditor's composition with, or time given to, the principal debtor discharges the surety unless he assents (Section 135). Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, Section 16(1): an arbitration clause is independent of the other terms of the contract.
