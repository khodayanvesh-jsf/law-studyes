## Meaning and Definition
Article 25 guarantees freedom of conscience and the right to freely profess, practise and propagate religion to whom?

Explanation. Article 25(1) guarantees this freedom to 'all persons', not just citizens, subject to public order, morality, health and the other provisions of Part III.

What test did the Supreme Court lay down in the Shirur Mutt case (1954) to determine what religious practices are protected under Article 25?

Explanation. Shirur Mutt (Commissioner, HRE v. Sri Lakshmindra Thirtha Swamiar) held that only practices genuinely essential and integral to a religion are constitutionally protected under Article 25.

Does Article 25(1) protect purely secular activities that happen to be associated with a religious practice?

Explanation. Article 25(2)(a) allows the State to regulate or restrict any economic, financial, political or other secular activity associated with religious practice.
