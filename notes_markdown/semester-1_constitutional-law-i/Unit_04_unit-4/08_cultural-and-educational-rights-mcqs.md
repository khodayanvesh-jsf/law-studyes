## Meaning and Definition
What right does Article 29(1) guarantee to any section of citizens with a distinct language, script or culture?

Explanation. Article 29(1) guarantees any section of citizens with a distinct language, script or culture the right to conserve it.

Is the right under Article 29(1) available only to religious minorities?

Explanation. Article 29(1) is not confined to religious minorities; it extends to any section of citizens, including linguistic groups, whether majority or minority in a given area.

What does Article 29(2) prohibit?

Explanation. Article 29(2) prohibits denying admission to any State-maintained or State-aided educational institution on grounds only of religion, race, caste, or language.
