# 4. Right to Constitutional Remedies
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester 1) | **Unit 4:** Unit 4
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/constitutional-law-i/right-to-constitutional-remedies/

## In One Minute
Article 32 is itself a Fundamental Right. It lets a person go straight to the Supreme Court when a Part III right is violated. The Court can issue directions, orders and writs, including the five named writs. Article 226 gives the High Courts a similar and wider power. Exams ask for the five writs, the difference between Articles 32 and 226, and compensation for violations. A problem question often asks which court and which remedy.

## Legal Provision
- Article 32(1): the right to move the Supreme Court by appropriate proceedings for the enforcement of the rights conferred by Part III is guaranteed. Article 32(2): the Supreme Court may issue directions, orders or writs to enforce any Part III right. They include writs in the nature of habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, quo warranto and certiorari, whichever is appropriate. Article 32(3): Parliament may by law empower any other court to exercise these powers within its local limits, without prejudice to the Supreme Court's powers. Article 32(4): the right guaranteed by this Article shall not be suspended except as otherwise provided for by this Constitution. Article 226(1): every High Court may issue directions, orders or writs to any person or authority, including a Government, within its territories. This is for the enforcement of Part III rights and for any other purpose. Article 359: during a Proclamation of Emergency, the President may suspend the right to move a court to enforce Part III rights named in the order, except Articles 20 and 21.

## Meaning and Definition
A right is only as good as its remedy. Article 32 supplies the remedy. A person whose Fundamental Right is violated does not have to climb through the trial and appeal courts first. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar told the Constituent Assembly on 9 December 1948 that without this Article the Constitution would be a nullity . He called it "the very soul of the Constitution and the very heart of it".

A writ is a court's written order to a person or authority. The five named writs are the main tools, but the Court may also issue any other direction or order that fits.

## Key Points to Remember
- Article 32 is itself a Fundamental Right and a guaranteed remedy. Five writs: habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, quo warranto. Article 32 is for Part III rights only. Article 226 also covers any other purpose. A merits dismissal by the High Court bars a fresh Article 32 petition (Daryao). Compensation is a public law remedy (Rudul Sah; Nilabati Behera). Judicial review under Articles 32, 226 and 227 is part of the basic structure. Article 359 cannot touch Articles 20 and 21.
