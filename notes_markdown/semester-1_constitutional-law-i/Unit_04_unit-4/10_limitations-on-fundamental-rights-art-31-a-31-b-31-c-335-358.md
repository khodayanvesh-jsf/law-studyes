## Meaning and Definition
In Basheshar Nath v. Commissioner of Income Tax (1959), what did the Supreme Court hold about a citizen's ability to waive a Fundamental Right?

Explanation. Basheshar Nath held that Fundamental Rights cannot be waived even by voluntary consent, since fundamental rights rest on public policy and there can be no estoppel against the Constitution.

What is the doctrine of severability, as applied to a law that partly violates a Fundamental Right?

Explanation. Under the doctrine of severability, only the offending, unconstitutional portion of a statute is struck down, provided it can be separated from the valid remainder without changing the law's essential character.

What is Article 13(2) of the Constitution's core mandate?

Explanation. Article 13(2) bars the State from making laws that take away or abridge Fundamental Rights, rendering any contravening law void to the extent of the contravention.
