## Meaning and Definition
The Directive Principles of State Policy are contained in which Part of the Constitution of India?

Explanation. The Directive Principles of State Policy are contained in Part IV, Articles 36 to 51, of the Constitution.

Which Article declares that the Directive Principles shall not be enforceable by any court?

Explanation. Article 37 makes the Directive Principles non-justiciable, while calling them fundamental in the governance of the country.

From which country's Constitution were the Directive Principles of State Policy borrowed?

Explanation. The Directive Principles in Part IV are generally traced to the Irish Constitution of 1937.
