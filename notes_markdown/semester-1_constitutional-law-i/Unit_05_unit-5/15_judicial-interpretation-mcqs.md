## Meaning and Definition
In M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (12 January 1988, AIR 1988 SC 1115, the Kanpur Nagar Mahapalika case), the Supreme Court relied on Article 51A(g) for which direction?

Explanation. The Court called protecting the natural environment one of the fundamental duties under Article 51A(g). It used that for the direction on environment lessons in schools.

Which of the following best describes how Article 51A(g) operated in the M.C. Mehta judgment of 12 January 1988?

Explanation. The judgment uses Article 51A(g) only for the environment education direction. It does not use Articles 21 or 51A(g) to close any industry.

For how long each week did the Court direct that lessons on protecting the natural environment be taught in the first ten classes?

Explanation. The Court directed teaching of at least one hour in a week, with free text books and a consideration of short training courses for teachers.
