## Meaning and Definition
The three-fold classification of Directive Principles into socialistic, Gandhian and liberal-intellectual groups is:

Explanation. Textbooks sort Articles 38 to 51 into three groups. The Constitution does not use these names, and books differ on a few Articles, such as Article 47.

Which two clauses of Article 39 later became central to the Article 31C controversy?

Explanation. Article 39(b) (distribution of material resources for the common good) and 39(c) (avoiding concentration of wealth) became the pivot of the Article 31C debate.

Article 40, directing the State to organise village panchayats, is regarded as reflecting which philosophy?

Explanation. Article 40 reflects Gandhian ideals of decentralised, village-level self-government, and is the constitutional seed of the 73rd Amendment.
