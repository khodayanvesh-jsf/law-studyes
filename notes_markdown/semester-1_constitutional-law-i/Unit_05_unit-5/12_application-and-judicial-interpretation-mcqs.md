## Meaning and Definition
Which case first established the 'harmonious construction' rule for reading Fundamental Rights and Directive Principles together?

Explanation. Chief Justice S.R. Das laid down the harmonious construction rule in the 1957 advisory opinion on the Kerala Education Bill.

In re The Kerala Education Bill reached the Supreme Court through which constitutional route?

Explanation. The President referred the Bill to the Supreme Court for an advisory opinion under Article 143.

In State of Kerala v. N.M. Thomas (1976), the Court read Article 16 together with which Directive Principle?

Explanation. The Court read Article 16's equality guarantee together with Article 46's promise to protect SC/ST educational and economic interests.
