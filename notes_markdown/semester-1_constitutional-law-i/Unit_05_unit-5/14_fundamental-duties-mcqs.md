## Meaning and Definition
The Fundamental Duties were inserted into the Constitution by which amendment?

Explanation. The 42nd Amendment, 1976 inserted Part IVA and Article 51A, on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee.

Which committee recommended the introduction of Fundamental Duties?

Explanation. The Sardar Swaran Singh Committee, appointed during the Emergency, recommended a chapter of Fundamental Duties.

Article 51A originally set out how many Fundamental Duties?

Explanation. The original 1976 text set out ten duties, clauses (a) to (j).
