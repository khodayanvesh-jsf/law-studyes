## Meaning and Definition
Article 20(1) protects a person against ex post facto criminal laws by providing that no person shall be:

Explanation. Article 20(1) bars conviction under a law that did not exist at the time the act was committed, and bars imposing a penalty greater than what the law in force at the time of commission prescribed.

Under the words of Article 20(1), a law made after the act that lowers the penalty for that offence is:

Explanation. Article 20(1) forbids conviction except for violating a law in force when the act was done, and a penalty greater than that law allowed. A later law that only lowers the penalty is outside those words.

Explanation. Section 358(2)(c) and (e) save penalties incurred under the repealed Code and allow proceedings for them to continue, which matches the protection in Article 20(1).
