# 4. Guarantee against Double Jeopardy
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester 1) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/constitutional-law-i/guarantee-against-double-jeopardy/

## In One Minute
Article 20(2) says no person shall be prosecuted and punished for the same offence more than once. The text needs a prosecution and a punishment for the same offence. Exams ask for the three conditions and for the contrast with section 337 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita. Remember that the text of Article 20(2) does not mention acquittal. The protection covers every person.

## Legal Provision
- Article 20(2): no person shall be prosecuted and punished for the same offence more than once. Article 359(1): during a Proclamation of Emergency, the right to move a court to enforce Part III rights may be suspended, except those in Articles 20 and 21.

## Meaning and Definition
Double jeopardy means being put in danger of punishment twice for one offence. The English maxims are autrefois acquit and autrefois convict (already acquitted, already convicted). The words of Article 20(2) are narrower than these maxims. The text needs both a prosecution and a punishment.

Is a second prosecution barred by Article 20(2)? How to read this: go down the boxes in order. A "No" answer sends you to the result beside the box. 1. Was the person prosecuted in the first proceeding? "prosecuted" No: Article 20(2) does not apply. 2. Did the first proceeding end in punishment? "prosecuted and punished" No: Article 20(2) does not apply, for example an acquittal. 3. Is the second proceeding for the same offence? same offence No: a different offence can be prosecuted. Yes to all: the second prosecution and punishment are barred.

## Key Points to Remember
- Article 20(2) needs prosecution and punishment for the same offence. The text says "same offence", not "same facts". Section 337 of the Sanhita is wider: it covers acquittal and, with exceptions, other offences on the same facts. Dismissal of a complaint or discharge is not an acquittal under section 337. Every person is covered. It cannot be suspended in an emergency (Article 359(1)).
