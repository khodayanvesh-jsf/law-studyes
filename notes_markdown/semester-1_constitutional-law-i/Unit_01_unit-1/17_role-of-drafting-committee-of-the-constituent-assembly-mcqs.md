## Meaning and Definition
The Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly was formed on:

Explanation. The Drafting Committee was formed on 29 August 1947 with seven members.

Who chaired the Drafting Committee and is widely regarded as the chief architect of the Indian Constitution?

Explanation. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Committee and is regarded as the chief architect of the Indian Constitution.

N. Madhava Rau joined the Drafting Committee to replace:

Explanation. N. Madhava Rau replaced B.L. Mitter after his resignation on grounds of ill health; T.T. Krishnamachari separately replaced D.P. Khaitan, who died in 1948.
