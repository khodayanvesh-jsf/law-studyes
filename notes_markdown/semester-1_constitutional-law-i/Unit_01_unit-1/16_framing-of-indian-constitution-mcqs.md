## Meaning and Definition
Who first formally proposed a constitution framed by Indians for India, in 1934?

Explanation. M.N. Roy first proposed a Constituent Assembly in 1934; the Indian National Congress officially demanded one in 1935.

The scheme by which the Constituent Assembly was actually elected and constituted came from:

Explanation. The Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946 provided that members from the provinces were chosen indirectly by the provincial legislative assemblies, and that the Indian States sent their own representatives.

The Constituent Assembly's original strength was 389 members. After Partition, this was reduced to:

Explanation. The Assembly was meant to have 389 members: 296 from British India and 93 from the Indian States. After the division of the country in August 1947, the strength became 299.
