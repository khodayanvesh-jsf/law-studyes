## Meaning and Definition
The sequence of events leading to the Government of India Act, 1935 was:

Explanation. Simon Commission Report (1930) → Round Table Conferences (1930–32) → White Paper (1933) → Government of India Act, 1935.

The All-India Federation proposed by the Government of India Act, 1935:

Explanation. The Act planned a Federation of the provinces and the Indian States that acceded (section 5). Too few States acceded, so the federal part never came into force.

Under the Government of India Act, 1935, dyarchy was:

Explanation. Provincial autonomy replaced dyarchy in the provinces. The Act also planned dyarchy at the Centre, but since the federation never began, that plan was never put into operation.
