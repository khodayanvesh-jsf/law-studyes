# 3. Constitutionalism and Constitutional Morality
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester 1) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1

## In One Minute
Constitutionalism means that government power is limited by law in practice, not only on paper. Constitutional morality means that officials, courts and citizens respect the values and procedures of the Constitution, even when they dislike the result. Exams ask for the meaning of both ideas, Ambedkar's speech of 4 November 1948 and three Supreme Court cases. In real life they decide who wins when a popular custom clashes with an individual's right.

## Legal Provision
- The phrases "constitutionalism" and "constitutional morality" do not appear in the text. They are developed from these provisions.
- Preamble : justice, liberty, equality and fraternity assuring the dignity of the individual. Article 13: a law that takes away or limits a Fundamental Right is void to that extent. Article 14: the State shall not deny any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws. Articles 32 and 226: the Supreme Court and the High Courts can issue writs to enforce rights. Article 50: the State shall take steps to separate the judiciary from the executive in the public services of the State. Article 75(3): the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the House of the People.

## Meaning and Definition
Constitutionalism (the idea that government power must be limited by law in practice) asks whether the rulers really obey the rules. A country can have a written constitution and yet lack constitutionalism, if its rulers ignore the document or its courts cannot enforce it.

Constitutional morality (a commitment to the values and procedures of the Constitution over personal views, popular opinion or convenience ) is the habit that makes constitutionalism work. B.R. Ambedkar brought the phrase into the Constituent Assembly on 4 November 1948. He quoted the historian George Grote, who called it a paramount reverence for the forms of the constitution.

Ambedkar then warned that constitutional morality is not a natural sentiment. He said it has to be cultivated , because Indians had yet to learn it. A democratic constitution survives only if officials and citizens follow constitutional methods even when they lose.

## Key Points to Remember
- Constitutionalism: power is limited by law in practice. Constitutional morality: respect for the Constitution's values and procedures over popular views. Source: Ambedkar, 4 November 1948, quoting Grote. Elements: supreme law, enforceable rights, separation of powers, independent judiciary, accountability. Cases: Manoj Narula (2014), Navtej Singh Johar (2018), Sabarimala (2018). Constitutional morality prevails over public morality when they conflict.
