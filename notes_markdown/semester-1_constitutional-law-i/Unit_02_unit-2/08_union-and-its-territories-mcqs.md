## Meaning and Definition
Which Article empowers Parliament to admit or establish new States into the Union?

Explanation. Article 2 empowers Parliament to admit into the Union, or establish, new States on such terms as it thinks fit.

Under Article 3, whose consent is required before Parliament can alter a State's boundaries?

Explanation. The affected State Legislature must be given a chance to express its views, but its views are not binding on Parliament.
