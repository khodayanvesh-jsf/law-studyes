## Meaning and Definition
Which Article of the Constitution declares that laws inconsistent with Fundamental Rights are void?

Explanation. Article 13 declares that any law inconsistent with Part III is void to the extent of the inconsistency.

The "doctrine of eclipse" applies to:

Explanation. The doctrine of eclipse applies only to pre-Constitution laws under Article 13(1) — such a law is dormant, not void, and can revive.

In Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India (1978), the Supreme Court held that "procedure established by law" under Article 21 must be:

Explanation. Maneka Gandhi held that the procedure under Article 21 must itself be fair, just, and reasonable, not arbitrary or oppressive.
