Explanation. Justice Bhagwati laid down a multi-factor test for determining whether a body is an instrumentality or agency of the State under Article 12.

Which case first introduced the "instrumentality of the State" theory through Justice Mathew's concurring opinion?

Explanation. Justice Mathew's concurring opinion in Sukhdev Singh v. Bhagatram (1975) introduced the "instrumentality of the State" theory.

In Pradeep Kumar Biswas v. Indian Institute of Chemical Biology (2002), which earlier case was overruled?

Explanation. Pradeep Kumar Biswas expressly overruled Sabhajit Tewary v. Union of India (1975), which had held CSIR was not "State."
