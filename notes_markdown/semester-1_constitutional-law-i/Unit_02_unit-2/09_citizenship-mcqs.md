## Meaning and Definition
Under Article 5, a person who has been ordinarily resident in India for how many years immediately before the Constitution's commencement could become a citizen?

Explanation. Article 5(c) required not less than five years' ordinary residence in India immediately preceding the Constitution's commencement.

A person who migrated from Pakistan to India on or after 19 July 1948 needed to:

Explanation. Migrants after 19 July 1948 needed to be registered as citizens, having been resident for at least six months immediately before the application.

Does the Constitution of India permit dual citizenship?

Explanation. Article 9 says that a person who has voluntarily acquired the citizenship of a foreign State is not a citizen under Articles 5, 6 or 8. Section 9 of the Citizenship Act, 1955 terminates Indian citizenship on such acquisition.
