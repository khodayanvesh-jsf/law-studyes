## Meaning and Definition
A contract of indemnity is defined in section ___ and a contract of guarantee in section ___ of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

Explanation. Section 124 defines a contract of indemnity. Section 126 defines a contract of guarantee.

How many persons are named in a contract of guarantee under section 126?

Explanation. Section 126 names the surety, the principal debtor and the creditor.
