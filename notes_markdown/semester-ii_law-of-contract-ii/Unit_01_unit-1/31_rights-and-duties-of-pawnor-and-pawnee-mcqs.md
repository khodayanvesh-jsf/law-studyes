## Meaning and Definition
Under section 173 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, the pawnee may retain the goods pledged for the debt and also for:

Explanation. Section 173 lets the pawnee retain the goods for the debt or promise, the interest of the debt, and all necessary expenses of possession or preservation.

In the absence of a contract to that effect, under section 174 the pawnee may retain the goods pledged for:

Explanation. Section 174 says the pawnee shall not, in the absence of a contract to that effect, retain the goods for any debt or promise other than that for which they are pledged.

Under section 174, a contract to retain the goods for subsequent advances made by the pawnee is:

Explanation. Section 174 says such a contract shall be presumed in regard to subsequent advances made by the pawnee, in the absence of anything to the contrary.
