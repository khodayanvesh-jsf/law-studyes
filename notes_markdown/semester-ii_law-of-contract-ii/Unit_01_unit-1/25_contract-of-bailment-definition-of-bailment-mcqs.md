## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 defines bailment?

Explanation. Section 148 defines bailment, bailor and bailee. Section 149 says how delivery to the bailee is made. Section 172 defines pledge.

A bailment is the delivery of goods by one person to another:

Explanation. Section 148 defines bailment as delivery of goods for some purpose, upon a contract that they shall, when the purpose is accomplished, be returned or otherwise disposed of according to the directions of the person delivering them.

The person delivering the goods in a bailment is called the:

Explanation. Under section 148, the person delivering the goods is the bailor.
