# 15. Pledge - Definition of pledge
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/pledge/

## In One Minute
A pledge is a special kind of bailment. Section 172 says the bailment of goods as security for payment of a debt or performance of a promise is called a pledge. The bailor is the pawnor . The bailee is the pawnee . Key points for the answer: the definition word for word, the two names, and the link to bailment.

## Meaning and Definition
The meaning of pledge (section 172)

Rule. A pledge is a bailment of goods as security for payment of a debt or performance of a promise. Take the definition in parts.

It is a bailment of goods. The goods are bailed as security. The security is for payment of a debt or performance of a promise.

Example. Anil borrows Rs 50,000 from Babu and hands his gold chain to Babu as security until he repays. This is a bailment of goods as security for payment of a debt. It is a pledge.

Pawnor and pawnee

Rule. The bailor in a pledge is the pawnor. The bailee is the pawnee.

Example. In the example above, Anil is the pawnor. Babu is the pawnee.

Pledge and bailment

Rule. Every pledge is a bailment, but not every bailment is a pledge. Section 172 calls a bailment a pledge only where the goods are bailed as security for a debt or a promise.

Example. Chandu gives his cycle to a repairer for repair. This is a bailment for repair, not a pledge, because the goods are not security for a debt or a promise.

Is it a pledge? How to read this: start at the top. 1. Are goods bailed (section 148)? 2. Are they bailed as security? 3. Is the security for payment of a debt or performance of a promise? If yes to all, it is a pledge (section 172). The bailor is the pawnor and the bailee is the pawnee.

## Key Points to Remember
- Pledge is the bailment of goods as security for payment of a debt or performance of a promise (section 172). The bailor is the pawnor. The bailee is the pawnee. Every pledge is a bailment. A bailment is a pledge only where the goods are security.
