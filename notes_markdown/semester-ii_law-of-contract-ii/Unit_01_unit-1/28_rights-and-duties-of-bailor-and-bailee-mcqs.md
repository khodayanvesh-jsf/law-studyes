## Meaning and Definition
Under section 151 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, the bailee must take as much care of the goods as:

Explanation. Section 151 sets the standard as the care a man of ordinary prudence would, under similar circumstances, take of his own goods of the same bulk, quality and value.

In the absence of any special contract, the bailee who has taken the care described in section 151 is:

Explanation. Section 152 says the bailee is not responsible for loss, destruction or deterioration if he has taken the care described in section 151.

A lends B a motor car for B's own drive. B lets his daughter C drive. The car is damaged during C's drive. Under section 154, B is:

Explanation. Section 154 makes the bailee liable for any damage arising to the goods from or during a use not according to the conditions of the bailment. Illustration (a) deals with a family member riding a horse with care.
