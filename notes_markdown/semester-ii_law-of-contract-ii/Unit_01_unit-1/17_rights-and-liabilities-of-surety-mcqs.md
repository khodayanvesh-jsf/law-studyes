## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 126 of the Indian Contract Act, the person to whom the guarantee is given is called the:

Explanation. Section 126 calls the person who gives the guarantee the surety, the person whose default is guaranteed the principal debtor, and the person to whom the guarantee is given the creditor.

Section 128 provides that, unless the contract provides otherwise, the liability of the surety is:

Explanation. Section 128 makes the surety's liability coextensive with the principal debtor's, unless the contract says otherwise. The Act's illustration holds a guarantor of a dishonoured bill liable for the amount, interest and charges.

Explanation. The Supreme Court held that the surety's liability was immediate and not deferred until the creditor exhausted his remedies against the principal debtor. It set the direction aside and ordered the surety to pay the Bank's costs.
