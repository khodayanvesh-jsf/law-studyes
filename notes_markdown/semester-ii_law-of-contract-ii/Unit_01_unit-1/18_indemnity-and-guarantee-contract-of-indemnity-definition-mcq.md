## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 defines a contract of indemnity?

Explanation. Section 124 defines a contract of indemnity. Section 125 deals with the rights of the indemnity-holder when sued, and section 126 defines a contract of guarantee.

A contract of indemnity is a contract by which one party promises to:

Explanation. Section 124 says a contract of indemnity is a contract by which one party promises to save the other from loss caused to him by the conduct of the promisor himself, or by the conduct of any other person.

In a contract of indemnity, the party who promises to save the other from loss is called the:

Explanation. The party who makes the promise is the promisor. The party who is promised protection is the promisee.
