# 10. Contract of Bailment - Definition of bailment
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/contract-of-bailment/

## In One Minute
A bailment is the delivery of goods by one person to another for some purpose, on a contract that the goods will be returned or otherwise disposed of as the person delivering directs. The person who delivers is the bailor . The person who receives is the bailee . Sections 148 and 149 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 define bailment and say how delivery is made. Key points for the answer: the definition word for word, the two names, the explanation to section 148 and the rule on delivery in section 149.

## Meaning and Definition
The meaning of bailment (section 148)

Rule. Bailment is the delivery of goods by one person to another for some purpose, upon a contract that the goods shall, when the purpose is accomplished, be returned or otherwise disposed of according to the directions of the person delivering them.

Goods are delivered by one person to another. The delivery is for some purpose. It is upon a contract that the goods will be returned, or disposed of as the bailor directs, when the purpose is accomplished.

Example. Ravi gives his scooter to a garage in Guntur for repair. The garage will return it when the repair is done. Ravi delivers goods to the garage for a purpose, on a contract that they will be returned. Ravi is the bailor and the garage is the bailee.

Bailor and bailee

Rule. The person delivering the goods is the bailor. The person to whom they are delivered is the bailee.

Example. Sita leaves her sewing machine with Lata for a week. Sita is the bailor. Lata is the bailee.

The explanation: a person already in possession

Rule. If a person already in possession of the goods of another contracts to hold them as a bailee, he thereby becomes the bailee. The owner becomes the bailor, although the goods may not have been delivered by way of bailment.

Example. Kiran already holds Mohan's bicycle. Kiran and Mohan now make a contract that Kiran will hold it as a bailee. By that contract Kiran becomes the bailee and Mohan the bailor, although the bicycle was not delivered by way of bailment.

How delivery is made (section 149)

Rule. Delivery may be made by doing anything which has the effect of putting the goods in the possession of the intended bailee, or of any person authorized to hold them on his behalf.

Example. Dev sends his goods to Esha's warehouse. Esha's authorized warehouse manager takes them in. The goods are in the possession of a person authorized to hold them on Esha's behalf. Delivery to Esha is complete.

The deliverer is the bailor and the receiver is the bailee.

## Key Points to Remember
- Section 148 defines bailment: delivery of goods by one person to another for some purpose, on a contract that they will be returned or disposed of as the person delivering directs. The person delivering is the bailor. The person receiving is the bailee. If a person already holds another's goods and contracts to hold them as a bailee, he becomes the bailee (explanation to section 148). Delivery means doing anything that puts the goods in the possession of the bailee, or of a person authorized to hold them for him (section 149). Pledge is bailment as security for a debt or a promise (section 172).
