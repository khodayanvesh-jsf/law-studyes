## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 lists the ways in which an agency is terminated?

Explanation. Section 201 lists revocation, renunciation, completion, death or unsound mind of either party, and insolvency of the principal.

Which of these does NOT terminate an agency under section 201?

Explanation. A change of the agent's residence is not among the ways listed in section 201.
