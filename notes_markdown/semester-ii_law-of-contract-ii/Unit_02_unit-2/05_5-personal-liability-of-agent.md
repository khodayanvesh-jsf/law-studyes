# 5. Personal liability of agent
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/personal-liability-of-agent/

## In One Minute
The general rule is that, in the absence of a contract to that effect, an agent cannot personally enforce contracts made for his principal, and is not personally bound by them (section 230). The Act presumes a contract to the contrary in three cases: an agent for a merchant resident abroad, an agent who does not disclose his principal's name, and a principal who, though disclosed, cannot be sued. Where the agent is personally liable, the third person may hold either him or his principal, or both, liable (section 233). A pretended agent is liable to compensate the other if the alleged employer does not ratify (section 235). Key points for the answer: the rule, the three cases, and sections 233, 235 and 236.

## Meaning and Definition
The general rule (section 230)

Rule. Without a contract to that effect, an agent cannot personally enforce contracts he makes for his principal and is not personally bound by them.

Example. Ravi, an agent, signs a contract with Sita for the sale of Chandu's goods, naming Chandu as principal. There is no contract that Ravi is personally bound. Ravi cannot sue Sita on the contract, and Sita cannot sue Ravi personally on it.

Three cases where a contract to the contrary is presumed (section 230)

Rule. The Act presumes that the agent is personally bound and may enforce in three cases.

The agent makes the contract for the sale or purchase of goods for a merchant resident abroad. The agent does not disclose the name of his principal. The principal, though disclosed, cannot be sued.

Example. Anil buys goods from Babu for a principal and does not tell Babu the principal's name. The Act presumes a contract that Anil is personally bound (case 2).

Choice of whom to hold liable (section 233)

Rule. Where the agent is personally liable, the third person may hold either the agent or the principal, or both, liable.

Example. A contracts to sell B 100 bales of cotton, and afterwards discovers that B was acting as agent for C. A may sue B or C, or both, for the price (the Act's illustration).

Pretended agent (section 235)

Rule. A person who untruly represents himself as the authorized agent of another, and thereby induces a third person to deal with him as agent, is liable to compensate the third person for any loss or damage incurred by so dealing, if his alleged employer does not ratify his acts.

Example. Dev falsely tells Esha that he is Farid's authorized agent and sells her goods in Farid's name. Farid does not ratify. Dev must compensate Esha for her loss from dealing with him.

Person falsely contracting as agent (section 236)

Rule. A person who contracted in the character of agent cannot require performance, if he was in reality acting on his own account and not as agent.

Is the agent personally liable? How to read this: start at the top. 1. Is there a contract that the agent is personally bound? If yes, he is personally bound. 2. If not, is the contract for goods for a merchant resident abroad, or is the principal's name not disclosed, or can the disclosed principal not be sued? If yes, such a contract is presumed (section 230). 3. Where the agent is personally liable, the third person may hold him, the principal, or both liable (section 233). 4. Was he not an authorized agent at all? He must compensate the third person if the alleged employer does not ratify (section 235).

## Key Points to Remember
- General rule: the agent neither enforces nor is bound by the contract, absent a contract to that effect (section 230). Presumed to the contrary: agent for a merchant abroad, undisclosed principal's name, principal who cannot be sued (section 230). Where the agent is personally liable, the third person may hold him, the principal or both liable (section 233). A pretended agent compensates the third person if the alleged employer does not ratify (section 235). A person acting on his own account as a false agent cannot require performance (section 236).
