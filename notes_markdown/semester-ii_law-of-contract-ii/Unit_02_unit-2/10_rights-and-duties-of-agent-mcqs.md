## Meaning and Definition
Under section 211 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, in the absence of directions from the principal, the agent must conduct the business according to:

Explanation. Section 211 says that in the absence of directions the agent must act according to the custom which prevails in business of the same kind at the place where he conducts it.

Under section 212, an agent must compensate his principal for:

Explanation. Section 212 requires compensation for direct consequences of the agent's neglect, want of skill or misconduct, but not for loss indirectly or remotely caused.

Under section 213, an agent must render proper accounts to his principal:

Explanation. Section 213 says an agent is bound to render proper accounts to his principal on demand.
