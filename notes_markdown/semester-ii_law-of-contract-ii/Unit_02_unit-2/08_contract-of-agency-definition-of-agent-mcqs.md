Which section of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 defines agent and principal?

Explanation. Section 182 defines agent and principal.

Explanation. Section 182 defines an agent as a person employed to do any act for another, or to represent another in dealings with third persons.

Explanation. Section 182 calls that person the principal.
