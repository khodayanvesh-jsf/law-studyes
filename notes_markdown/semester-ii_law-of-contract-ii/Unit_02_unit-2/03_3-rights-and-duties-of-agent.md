# 3. Rights and duties of Agent
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/rights-and-duties-of-agent/

## In One Minute
An agent must follow his principal's directions or the custom of the place (section 211), use reasonable skill and diligence (section 212), render accounts (section 213), communicate in difficulty (section 214), and pay over what he receives for the principal (section 218). He must not deal on his own account in the agency business without the principal's consent (sections 215 and 216). The agent may retain what is due to him out of sums received (section 217), has a lien (section 221), and is entitled to be indemnified (sections 222 and 223). Key points for the answer: each duty and right with its section and the Act's illustrations.

## Meaning and Definition
Duties of the agent

Rule. The agent has these duties.

Follow directions or custom (section 211). Loss on departure is made good, and profit is accounted for. Skill and diligence (section 212). As much skill as persons in similar business generally have, unless the principal knows of his want of skill. Reasonable diligence always. Compensation for direct, not remote, consequences of his neglect. Accounts (section 213). Proper accounts on demand. Communicate (section 214). In difficulty, use all reasonable diligence to communicate and seek instructions. Do not deal on own account (sections 215 and 216). Consent and disclosure are needed. Without them, the principal may repudiate if a material fact was dishonestly concealed or the dealing was disadvantageous (section 215). If the agent deals for himself without the principal's knowledge, the principal may claim the benefit (section 216). Pay over sums received (section 218), after the deductions in section 217.

Example. Ravi directs his agent Sita to buy a particular house for him. Sita tells Ravi it cannot be bought and buys it herself. Ravi may compel Sita to sell it to him at the price she paid (section 216, the Act's illustration).

Rights of the agent

Rule. The agent has these rights.

Retainer (section 217). He may retain, out of sums received on the principal's account, moneys due for advances, expenses properly incurred and his remuneration. Remuneration (sections 219 and 220). In the absence of a special contract it is due on completion of the act. He may detain moneys received for goods sold. An agent guilty of misconduct has no remuneration for the part misconducted. Lien (section 221). In the absence of a contract to the contrary, he may retain the principal's goods, papers and other property until the amount due for commission, disbursements and services is paid or accounted for. Indemnity (sections 222 to 225). The principal must indemnify him against the consequences of lawful acts done in exercise of his authority (section 222) and of acts done in good faith, though they injure third persons (section 223). He is not entitled to indemnity for a criminal act (section 224). The principal must compensate injury caused by his own neglect or want of skill (section 225).

Example. Anil, a broker, buys oil for Babu on Babu's orders. Babu repudiates the contract, and the seller sues Anil, who defends unsuccessfully and pays damages and costs. Babu must indemnify Anil for the damages, costs and expenses (section 222, illustration (b)).

When the agent deals on his own account How to read this: start at the top. 1. The agent deals on his own account in the business of the agency. 2. Did he first obtain the principal's consent and tell him all material circumstances? If not, the principal may repudiate if a material fact was dishonestly concealed or the dealing was disadvantageous (section 215). 3. Did he deal without the principal's knowledge, instead of on the principal's account? The principal may claim any benefit the agent gained (section 216).

## Key Points to Remember
- The agent follows directions or custom (section 211) and uses skill and diligence (section 212). Accounts on demand (section 213). Communicate in difficulty (section 214). No dealing on his own account without consent and disclosure (sections 215, 216). Retainer (section 217). Remuneration (sections 219, 220). Lien (section 221). Indemnity (sections 222, 223). Not for a criminal act (section 224). Compensation for the principal's neglect (section 225).
