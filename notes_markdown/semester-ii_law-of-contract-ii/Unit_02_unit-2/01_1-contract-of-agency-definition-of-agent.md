# 1. Contract of Agency-Definition of Agent
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 2:** Unit 2
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/contract-of-agency/

## In One Minute
An agent is a person employed to do any act for another, or to represent another in dealings with third persons. The person for whom the act is done, or who is represented, is the principal (section 182). Any person of the age of majority and of sound mind may employ an agent (section 183). No consideration is needed to create an agency (section 185). Key points for the answer: the definition, who may employ an agent, who may be an agent, and no consideration.

## Meaning and Definition
Agent and principal (section 182)

Rule. An agent is a person employed to do any act for another, or to represent another in dealings with third persons. The other person is the principal. Take the definition in two parts: employed to do an act for another, or employed to represent another with third persons.

Example. Ravi lives in Hyderabad and owns a shop in Guntur. He employs Sita to run the shop and to buy goods for it from suppliers. Sita is employed to do acts for Ravi and to represent him in dealings with suppliers. Sita is the agent and Ravi is the principal.

Who may employ an agent (section 183)

Rule. Any person who is of the age of majority according to the law to which he is subject, and who is of sound mind, may employ an agent.

Example. Anil is an adult of sound mind. He may employ an agent. A person who is not of the age of majority is outside section 183.

Who may be an agent (section 184)

Rule. As between the principal and third persons, any person may become an agent. But a person who is not of the age of majority and of sound mind cannot become an agent so as to be responsible to his principal according to the Act's provisions.

Example. Babu employs a minor, Chandu, as his agent to buy goods. As between Babu and the third persons, any person may become an agent (section 184). The section also says a person who is not of the age of majority and of sound mind cannot become an agent so as to be responsible to his principal under the Act's provisions.

No consideration (section 185)

Rule. No consideration is necessary to create an agency.

Example. Dev asks his friend Esha to collect a parcel for him, and offers her nothing in return. If Esha accepts and acts for him, an agency can still arise. Section 185 does not require consideration.

Who can be a principal and an agent How to read this: follow each line to the section. 1. The principal: of the age of majority and of sound mind (section 183). 2. The agent: employed to do an act for the principal or to represent him with third persons (section 182). As between the principal and third persons, any person may be an agent. To be responsible to his principal under the Act, he must be of the age of majority and of sound mind (section 184). 3. Consideration is not necessary (section 185).

## Key Points to Remember
- Agent: a person employed to do any act for another or to represent another in dealings with third persons (section 182). Any person of the age of majority and of sound mind may employ an agent (section 183). As between the principal and third persons, any person may be an agent. To be responsible to his principal, he must be of the age of majority and of sound mind (section 184). No consideration is necessary (section 185).
