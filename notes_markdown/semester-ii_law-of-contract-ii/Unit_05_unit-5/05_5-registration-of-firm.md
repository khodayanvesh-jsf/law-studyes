# 5. Registration of firm
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/registration-of-firm/

## In One Minute
Under section 58 of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932, the registration of a firm may be effected at any time by sending or delivering to the Registrar of the area a statement in the prescribed form with the prescribed fee. The statement gives the firm name, the principal place of business, other places of business, the date each partner joined, the partners' names and addresses, and the duration of the firm. When the Registrar is satisfied, he records an entry in the Register of Firms and files the statement (section 59). Later changes can be recorded (sections 60 to 63), mistakes rectified (section 64), and the Register inspected (section 66). Key points for the answer: who registers, what the statement contains, and the later steps.

## Meaning and Definition
How a firm is registered (sections 57 to 59)

Rule. The firm sends or delivers to the Registrar of the area a statement with the prescribed fee. It states the firm name, the principal place of business, other places of business, the date each partner joined, the partners' full names and permanent addresses, and the duration of the firm. All the partners or their specially authorised agents sign it, and each signatory verifies it. When the Registrar is satisfied that section 58 is complied with, he records an entry in the Register of Firms and files the statement.

Example. Rohan, Seema and Tara run a firm called "Rohan and Company" from a shop in Pune. They send the Registrar a signed and verified statement with the fee, giving the particulars listed in section 58(1). The Registrar records an entry in the Register of Firms (section 59).

Restrictions on the firm name (section 58(3))

Rule. A firm name may not contain "Crown", "Emperor", "Empress", "Empire", "Imperial", "King", "Queen", "Royal", or words expressing or implying Government sanction, approval or patronage, unless the State Government consents in writing.

Recording later changes (sections 60 to 63)

Rule. A registered firm may have the Register updated.

Change of firm name or principal place of business: a statement with the fee, signed and verified as in section 58 (section 60). Opening or closing of a branch: intimation by any partner or agent (section 61). Change of a partner's name or address: intimation by any partner or agent (section 62). Change in the constitution of the firm or its dissolution: notice by an incoming, continuing or outgoing partner, or by a person who was a partner just before dissolution, or an authorised agent. A minor's election on attaining majority is also noticed to the Registrar (section 63).

Rectification, amendment, inspection, copies and evidence (sections 64 to 68)

Rule. The Registrar may always rectify mistakes to bring an entry into conformity with the filed documents, and may rectify a mistake in a document on the application of all who signed it (section 64). A Court may direct a consequential amendment (section 65). The Register and filed documents are open to inspection on fee (section 66). The Registrar furnishes certified copies on application and fee (section 67). A recorded statement, intimation or notice is conclusive proof against its signatories, and a certified copy proves the registration and contents (section 68).

Penalty and rules (sections 70 and 71)

Rule. A person who signs a statement or notice with a particular he knows to be false or does not believe to be true, or with particulars he knows to be incomplete, is punishable with up to three months' imprisonment, or fine, or both (section 70). The State Government makes rules under section 71.

Steps in registration How to read this: start at the top. 1. Prepare a statement with the particulars in section 58(1), signed by all partners or their authorised agents and verified. Send or deliver it with the fee to the Registrar of the area. 2. The Registrar is satisfied that section 58 is complied with. He records an entry in the Register of Firms and files the statement (section 59). 3. Later changes. Notified by statement or intimation, and recorded (sections 60 to 63). Mistakes are rectified (section 64).

## Key Points to Remember
- Registration may be effected at any time by a statement with the fee to the Registrar of the area (section 58). Six particulars: name, principal place, other places, date each partner joined, partners' names and addresses, duration. Signed by all partners or their authorised agents and verified. The Registrar records an entry in the Register of Firms (section 59). Changes are recorded (sections 60 to 63). Mistakes are rectified (section 64). False particulars: up to three months' imprisonment, or fine, or both (section 70).
