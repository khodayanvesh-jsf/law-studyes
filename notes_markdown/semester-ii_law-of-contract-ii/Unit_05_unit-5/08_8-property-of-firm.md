# 8. Property of firm
**Subject:** Law of Contracts – II (Semester II) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-contract-ii/property-of-firm/

## In One Minute
Subject to contract between the partners, the property of the firm includes all property and rights and interests in property originally brought into the stock of the firm, or acquired, by purchase or otherwise, by or for the firm, or for the purposes and in the course of the business of the firm. It also includes the goodwill of the business (section 14). Unless the contrary intention appears, property acquired with money belonging to the firm is deemed to have been acquired for the firm (section 14). Subject to contract, the property of the firm shall be held and used by the partners exclusively for the purposes of the business (section 15). Key points for the answer: what is included, the presumption for property bought with firm money, and the duty of exclusive use.

## Meaning and Definition
What is the firm's property (section 14)

Rule. Subject to the partners' contract, the firm's property includes three groups.

Property and rights and interests in property originally brought into the stock of the firm. Property and rights and interests acquired, by purchase or otherwise, by or for the firm, or for the purposes and in the course of its business. The goodwill of the business.

Example. Anil and Bina start a bakery. Anil brings an oven into the firm's stock, and the firm later buys a delivery van. Both the oven and the van are property of the firm, and so is the goodwill of the bakery (section 14).

Property bought with the firm's money (section 14)

Rule. Unless the contrary intention appears, property and rights and interests in property acquired with money belonging to the firm are deemed to have been acquired for the firm.

Example. Chandan buys a plot in his own name using money from the firm's account. Unless the contrary intention appears, the plot is deemed acquired for the firm.

Use of the firm's property (section 15)

Rule. Subject to the partners' contract, the property of the firm shall be held and used by the partners exclusively for the purposes of the business.

Is it the firm's property? How to read this: start at the top. Every step is subject to a contract between the partners. 1. Was it originally brought into the stock of the firm? It is firm property (section 14). 2. Was it acquired by or for the firm, or for the purposes and in the course of its business? It is firm property (section 14). 3. Was it acquired with money belonging to the firm? It is deemed acquired for the firm, unless the contrary intention appears (section 14). 4. How may it be used? Held and used by the partners exclusively for the purposes of the business (section 15).

## Key Points to Remember
- Firm property: what is brought into the stock, what is acquired by or for the firm or for its business, and the goodwill (section 14). Property bought with firm money is deemed bought for the firm, unless the contrary intention appears (section 14). Held and used exclusively for the purposes of the business, subject to contract (section 15).
