## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Hindu Marriage Act makes it the court's duty to attempt reconciliation before granting relief?

Explanation. Section 23(2) makes it the court's duty to attempt reconciliation wherever possible.

Under Section 23(3), the court may adjourn proceedings for reconciliation for a period ordinarily not exceeding:

Explanation. Section 23(3) permits adjournment for reconciliation, ordinarily not exceeding fifteen days.

Under Section 13B(1), the parties must have been living separately for a period of:

Explanation. Section 13B(1) requires the parties to have been living separately for a period of one year or more.
