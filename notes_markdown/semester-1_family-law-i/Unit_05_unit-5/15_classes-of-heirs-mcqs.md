## Meaning and Definition
"Per stirpes" distribution means:

Explanation. Per stirpes fixes the number of shares by branch, before those shares are split among individuals.

Under section 10, Rule 1, if the intestate leaves more than one widow, the widows take:

Explanation. Rule 1 of section 10 gives the widow, or all the widows together, one share.

Where is the father placed in the Schedule?

Explanation. The father is in class II, entry I. The mother is in class I.
