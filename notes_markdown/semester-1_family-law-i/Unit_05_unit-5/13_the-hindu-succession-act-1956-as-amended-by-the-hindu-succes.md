## Meaning and Definition
Section 2 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956 applies to:

Explanation. Section 2 extends the Act to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Sikhs, and to anyone not shown to be governed by another personal law.

Which provision did section 2 of the Hindu Succession (Amendment) Act, 2005 omit?

Explanation. Section 4(2) was omitted by Act 39 of 2005, section 2, with effect from 9 September 2005.

Which section preserves a Hindu's right to make a will?

Explanation. Section 30 preserves every Hindu's right to dispose of property by will, overriding the intestate rules.
