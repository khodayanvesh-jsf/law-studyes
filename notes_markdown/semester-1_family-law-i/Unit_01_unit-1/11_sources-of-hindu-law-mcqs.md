## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 2 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955, which of the following is NOT excluded from the application of Hindu law?

Explanation. Section 2 excludes Muslims, Christians, Parsis, and Jews. Sikhs are expressly brought under Hindu law by the same Section.

According to Vineeta Sharma (para 18), basic Hindu law emanates from which ancient sources?

Explanation. The Supreme Court recorded in Vineeta Sharma (para 18) that basic Hindu law emanates from the Vedas and past shrutis and smritis.

Under section 3(a) of the Hindu Marriage Act, a custom must be:

Explanation. Section 3(a) requires a custom to be ancient, certain and reasonable and not against public policy.
