## Meaning and Definition
Which of these correctly describes 'severance of status' in Hindu law?

Explanation. Severance of status is a change in legal relationship only — it needs no physical division of property at all.

In Addagada Raghavamma v. Addagada Chenchamma (1964), the Supreme Court held that severance of status requires:

Explanation. The Court held severance requires a clear, unequivocal declaration of intent to separate, which must be communicated to the other affected coparceners.

'Division by metes and bounds' refers to:

Explanation. Division by metes and bounds is the actual, physical division of property into specific, individual shares — the second stage of partition.
