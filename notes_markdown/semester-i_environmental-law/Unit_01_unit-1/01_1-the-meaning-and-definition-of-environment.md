# 1. The meaning and definition of environment
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/meaning-and-definition-of-environment/

## In One Minute
Environment means everything that surrounds a living being and affects its life. The law gives it a fixed meaning in section 2(a) of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. That meaning covers water, air and land, and the links between them and all living beings and property. Every pollution case starts from this definition, so exams often begin with it.

## Legal Provision
- The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 is a Central Act. The text below is checked against the Bare Act page on this site.
- Section 2(a): "environment" includes water, air and land and the inter-relationship which exists among and between water, air and land, and human beings, other living creatures, plants, micro-organism and property. Section 2(b): an "environmental pollutant" is any solid, liquid or gaseous substance present in such concentration as may be, or tend to be, injurious to environment. Section 2(c): "environmental pollution" is the presence in the environment of any environmental pollutant. Section 3(1): the Central Government can take all measures it thinks necessary to protect and improve the quality of the environment and to prevent, control and abate pollution.

## Meaning and Definition
In everyday speech, environment means the surroundings of a person or a living being. The word comes from the French environner , which means to encircle . It includes living things such as plants and animals, and non-living things such as air, water and soil.

The law needs a firmer meaning, so section 2(a) of the Act supplies one. The key feature is that the definition names the inter-relationship and not only the physical parts. Water, air and land are one connected system with people, other living creatures, plants, micro-organisms and property.

## Key Points to Remember
- Source: section 2(a), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. Parts: water, air, land, their inter-relationship, living beings, property. The Act's word is "micro-organism". The definition begins with "includes", so it is not a closed list. Environmental pollution is the presence of an environmental pollutant in the environment (section 2(c)). Ecology and ecosystem are not defined in the Act.
