# 12. Environmental degradation and pollution
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/environmental-degradation-and-pollution/

## In One Minute
Environmental degradation is the overall decline in the quality of the environment. Pollution is one main way it happens: harmful substances enter the environment and cause harm. Indian statutes define pollution in their own words. Exams ask for the two meanings, the difference between them and the statutory definitions.

## Legal Provision
- The texts below are checked against the Bare Act pages on this site.
- Section 2(b), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: an "environmental pollutant" is any solid, liquid or gaseous substance present in such concentration as may be, or tend to be, injurious to environment. Section 2(c), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: "environmental pollution" is the presence in the environment of any environmental pollutant. Section 2(e), Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974: water "pollution" is such contamination of water, or alteration of its physical, chemical or biological properties, or discharge of sewage , trade effluent or any other liquid, gaseous or solid substance into water, as may or is likely to create a nuisance or make the water harmful to public health or safety, to its legitimate uses, or to the life and health of animals, plants or aquatic organisms. Section 2(a) and (b), Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981: an "air pollutant" is any solid, liquid or gaseous substance (including noise) present in the atmosphere in such concentration as may be or tend to be injurious to human beings, other living creatures, plants, property or environment. "Air pollution" is the presence in the atmosphere of any air pollutant.

## Meaning and Definition
Environmental degradation is the decline in the quality of the environment through loss of resources, destruction of ecosystems and loss of biodiversity. It is the wider process.

Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances into the environment, resulting in harm to living things and ecosystems. It happens when these substances build up beyond what the environment can absorb or make harmless.

## Key Points to Remember
- Degradation: overall decline in environmental quality. Pollution: harmful substances in the environment beyond its capacity to absorb. 1986 Act: pollutant (section 2(b)), pollution (section 2(c)). Water Act section 2(e): contamination likely to harm water or its uses. Air Act section 2(a): air pollutant includes noise.
