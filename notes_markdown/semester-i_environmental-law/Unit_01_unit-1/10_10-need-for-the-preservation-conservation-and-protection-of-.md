# 10. Need for the preservation, conservation and protection of environment
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/need-for-preservation-conservation-and-protection-of-environment/

## In One Minute
People need clean air, water and soil to live. Preservation, conservation and protection are three different ways of keeping the environment fit for them. Preservation keeps something untouched. Conservation allows careful use. Protection uses law to stop harm. Exams ask for the three meanings and the reasons the need is urgent.

## Legal Provision
- Article 48A, Constitution of India: the State shall endeavour to protect and improve the environment and to safeguard the forests and wild life of the country. Article 51A(g), Constitution of India: every citizen has a duty to protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers and wild life. Every citizen must also have compassion for living creatures. Article 21, Constitution of India: no person shall be deprived of life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law. Courts have read a healthy environment into this right. Section 3(1), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: the Central Government can take all measures it thinks necessary to protect and improve the environment.

## Meaning and Definition
The three words are often used as if they meant the same. In environmental law they do not.

Preservation : keeping a resource or area untouched, usually by keeping human use out. Conservation : planned and sustainable use of resources, balancing present needs with future ones. Protection : active legal and administrative steps to prevent harm, such as pollution control, enforcement and penalties.

Keeping the environment fit for life Preservation Leave it untouched. Tool: strict reserve or core zone. Conservation Use it sustainably. Tool: management plans and limits on use. Protection Stop harm by law. Tool: statutes, penalties, pollution control bodies.

## Key Points to Remember
- Preservation: untouched. Conservation: sustainable use. Protection: legal prevention of harm. Reasons: survival, depletion, irreversible loss, future generations, shared harm. Article 48A binds the State. Article 51A(g) binds citizens. Article 21 includes a healthy environment. Public trust: the State holds natural resources for the public.
