## Meaning and Definition
The ozone layer is located in which layer of the atmosphere?

Explanation. The ozone layer sits in the stratosphere, roughly 10 to 50 kilometres above the Earth's surface.

Which substances are the primary cause of ozone depletion?

Explanation. Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) and halons are the primary causes of ozone depletion — they release chlorine and bromine atoms that destroy ozone molecules.

Global warming is best defined as:

Explanation. Global warming is the long-term rise in Earth's average surface temperature, caused by the accumulation of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.
