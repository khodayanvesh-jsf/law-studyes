## Meaning and Definition
The biosphere is best described as:

Explanation. The biosphere is the global sum of all ecosystems on Earth — the zone where the lithosphere, hydrosphere, and atmosphere overlap and support life.

Explanation. A biome is a large, climate-defined geographical region with its own characteristic plant and animal communities — much larger in scale than a single ecosystem.

Which of the following is an example of a terrestrial biome?

Explanation. Savanna is a terrestrial biome. Estuaries, marine biomes, and freshwater biomes are all aquatic biomes.

The Sundarbans is a well-known example of which kind of aquatic biome?

Explanation. The Sundarbans is an estuary — a transitional zone where freshwater from rivers mixes with saltwater from the sea, home to mangrove forests.
