# 1. The law relating to the preservation, conservation and protection of forests
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/law-relating-to-preservation-conservation-and-protection-of-forests/

## In One Minute
The Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 stops a State from using forest land for other purposes without the Central Government's prior approval. Since 2023 its short title is the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam. In the Godavarman case the Supreme Court gave "forest" its wide dictionary meaning. The 2023 Amendment now says which land the Act covers and which it does not. Questions often ask about both.

## Legal Provision
- The main provisions are these. The text of the 2023 Amendment is from the Gazette of India of 4 August 2023.
- Section 2 of the 1980 Act: no State Government or other authority may make certain orders without the prior approval of the Central Government. The orders are for de-reservation of a forest, for use of forest land for a non-forest purpose, for assigning forest land by lease to a private person or an outside organisation, and for clearing naturally grown trees for reafforestation. Sections 3, 3A and 3B of the 1980 Act: the Advisory Committee, the penalty of simple imprisonment up to fifteen days, and offences by Government departments and authorities. Section 1A (inserted in 2023): which land the Act covers and which it does not. Section 3C (inserted in 2023): the Central Government may give directions to carry out the Act. Article 48A and Article 51A(g): the State must safeguard forests, and every citizen must protect and improve forests, lakes, rivers and wild life. Entry 17A of the Concurrent List: forests, inserted by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976.

## Meaning and Definition
Forest law decides who may change the use of forest land. The Act does not ban every use. It puts the decision in the hands of the Central Government, so that a State cannot hand forest land to others on its own.

The Act's own Explanation says "non-forest purpose" means breaking up or clearing forest land for tea, coffee, spices, rubber, palms, oil-bearing plants, horticultural crops or medicinal plants, or for any purpose other than reafforestation. Work to conserve forests and wild life does not count.

## Key Points to Remember
- Section 2: no use of forest land for other purposes without prior Central approval. Section 3 (Advisory Committee), section 3A (simple imprisonment up to fifteen days), section 3B (head of department deemed guilty unless he proves no knowledge or due diligence). Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act, 2023 (Act 15 of 2023): renamed the Act Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam. Section 1A: notified forest, plus land recorded as forest on or after 25 October 1980. Exemptions include border land within 100 km for strategic linear projects. Godavarman (1996): dictionary meaning of forest. Kamal Nath: public trust doctrine.
