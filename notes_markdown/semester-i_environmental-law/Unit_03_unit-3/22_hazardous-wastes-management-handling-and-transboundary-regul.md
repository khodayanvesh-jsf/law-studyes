## Meaning and Definition
India's domestic hazardous waste regulation is implemented through:

Explanation. There is no standalone hazardous waste Act — the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016 are made under Sections 6/8/25 of the EPA, 1986.

The currently governing regulation for hazardous waste management is:

Explanation. The 2016 Rules were made under sections 6, 8 and 25 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, in supersession of the 2008 Rules.

The 2016 Rules widened their scope beyond strictly hazardous waste to also cover:

Explanation. Rule 3 defines "other wastes" as the wastes in Parts B and D of Schedule III, including such waste generated in India. The Rules cover them alongside hazardous waste.
