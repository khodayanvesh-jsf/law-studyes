## Meaning and Definition
The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 was enacted under which constitutional mechanism?

Explanation. Since water is a State List subject, Parliament used Article 252 after two or more States passed resolutions requesting central legislation.

Which bodies are established under Sections 3 and 4 of the Water Act, 1974?

Explanation. Sections 3 and 4 establish the CPCB and SPCBs, which also administer the Air Act, 1981.

Before establishing an industry likely to discharge trade effluent into a stream, Sections 25/26 require:

Explanation. Sections 25/26 require prior State Board consent — Consent to Establish before construction, and Consent to Operate before actual operation.
