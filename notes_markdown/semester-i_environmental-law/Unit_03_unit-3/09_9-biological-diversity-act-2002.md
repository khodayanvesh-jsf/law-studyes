# 9. Biological Diversity Act, 2002
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/biological-diversity-act-2002/

## In One Minute
The Biological Diversity Act, 2002 gives effect to the Convention on Biological Diversity. It controls who may take Indian biological resources and the knowledge linked to them. It requires fair and equitable sharing of the benefits. It has three levels: the National Biodiversity Authority, State Biodiversity Boards and local Biodiversity Management Committees. Foreign persons need approval. Indian users give prior intimation.

## Legal Provision
- The main sections, read from the Act as stored on this site (as amended up to Act 10 of 2023), are these.
- Preamble: India is a Party to the Convention on Biological Diversity signed at Rio de Janeiro on 5 June 1992, in force from 29 December 1993. The Act also refers to the Nagoya Protocol adopted on 29 October 2010. Section 3: approval of the National Biodiversity Authority is needed to obtain a biological resource or associated knowledge for research, commercial use, bio-survey or bio-utilisation. Section 4: results of research cannot be transferred to such persons without the Authority's written approval. Section 6: intellectual property rights based on Indian biological resources. Section 7: prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board for commercial use. Section 8: the National Biodiversity Authority, with its head office at Chennai. Section 21: benefit sharing. Section 22: State Biodiversity Boards. Section 41: Biodiversity Management Committees. Section 55: penalty.

## Meaning and Definition
The Act protects biological resources and the knowledge that goes with them. Its aim is conservation, sustainable use and fair and equitable benefit sharing . A company that profits from an Indian plant must share the gain with the people and the country that preserved it.

The wrong the Act addresses is biopiracy , the taking of biological resources or traditional knowledge without permission or fair return . The wider idea of biodiversity is the variety of living things.

Three levels of the Act How to read this: the outer box is the national body. Each inner box works under the one around it. National Biodiversity Authority approvals under sections 3, 4 and 6; benefit sharing under section 21 State Biodiversity Boards regulate access under section 7 by prior intimation Biodiversity Management Committees in every local body: conservation and documentation (section 41)

## Key Points to Remember
- Gives effect to the Convention on Biological Diversity (Rio, 1992). Section 3: approval for non-citizens, NRIs and foreign-controlled bodies. Section 7: prior intimation to the State Board for commercial use. Section 6: intellectual property rights. Section 21: benefit sharing. Three tiers: Authority (Chennai), State Boards, Management Committees. Section 55: penalty of Rs 1 lakh to Rs 50 lakh.
