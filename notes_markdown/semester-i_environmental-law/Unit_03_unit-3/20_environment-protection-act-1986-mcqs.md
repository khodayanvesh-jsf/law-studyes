## Meaning and Definition
According to its Preamble, the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 implements the decisions of which conference?

Explanation. The Preamble says the Act implements the decisions of the Stockholm Conference of June 1972 on protecting and improving the human environment. It is an umbrella Act, because it reaches every kind of pollution and not one medium alone.

Section 3 of the EPA, 1986 is significant because it:

Explanation. Section 3's broad, general power is the legal basis for the EIA Notification, CRZ Notifications, Hazardous Waste Rules, Plastic/Solid Waste Rules, and most other Indian environmental subordinate legislation.

How does the Central Government's Section 5 direction power differ from the State Board's powers under the Water/Air Acts?

Explanation. Section 5 is exercised directly by the Central Government (not a State Board) and is broader, not confined to a single pollution medium.
