## Meaning and Definition
The Biological Diversity Act, 2002 implements India's obligations under which international instrument?

Explanation. The Act implements India's ratification of the Convention on Biological Diversity, 1992, adopted at the Rio Earth Summit.

Which body maintains the People's Biodiversity Register at the grassroots level?

Explanation. Biodiversity Management Committees, constituted by local bodies under Section 41, maintain People's Biodiversity Registers documenting local resources and traditional knowledge.

Under Section 7, an Indian citizen seeking to obtain a biological resource for commercial utilisation must:

Explanation. Section 7 requires only prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board for Indian citizens/entities seeking commercial utilisation, a lighter requirement than Section 3's NBA approval for foreign entities.
