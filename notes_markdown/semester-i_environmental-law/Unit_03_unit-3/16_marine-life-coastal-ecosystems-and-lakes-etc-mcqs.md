## Meaning and Definition
Under the 1976 Maritime Zones Act, India's territorial waters extend up to:

Explanation. Territorial waters extend twelve nautical miles from the nearest point of the baseline (section 3 of the 1976 Act).

In its exclusive economic zone, a coastal State has:

Explanation. India has an exclusive economic zone in which a coastal State has sovereign rights over resources and a duty to protect the marine environment.

The Coastal Regulation Zone Notification is issued under:

Explanation. The CRZ Notification is delegated legislation under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.
