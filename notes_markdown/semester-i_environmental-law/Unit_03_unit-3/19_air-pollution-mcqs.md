## Meaning and Definition
The Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 was enacted under which constitutional provision?

Explanation. Since 'air' appears in no legislative list, Parliament used Article 253 (power to implement international agreements) to enact the Air Act following the Stockholm Conference, 1972.

Which body administers the Air Act, 1981 at the state level?

Explanation. Sections 3-5 of the Air Act do not create a new Board; the existing SPCB (and CPCB) from the Water Act, 1974 are given additional air-pollution functions.

Under Section 19, who may declare an area as an Air Pollution Control Area?

Explanation. Section 19 empowers the State Government, after consulting the State Board, to declare an Air Pollution Control Area and restrict fuels/appliances within it.
