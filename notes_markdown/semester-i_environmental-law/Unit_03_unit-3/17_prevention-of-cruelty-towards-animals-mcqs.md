## Meaning and Definition
Which Fundamental Duty requires citizens to have compassion for living creatures?

Explanation. Article 51A(g) makes compassion for living creatures a Fundamental Duty.

Prevention of cruelty to animals is Entry 17 of which list in the Seventh Schedule?

Explanation. Entry 17 of the Concurrent List covers prevention of cruelty to animals.

Which section of the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 lays down the duty of care of persons in charge of an animal?

Explanation. Section 3 imposes the duty to ensure the well-being of an animal and prevent unnecessary pain or suffering.
