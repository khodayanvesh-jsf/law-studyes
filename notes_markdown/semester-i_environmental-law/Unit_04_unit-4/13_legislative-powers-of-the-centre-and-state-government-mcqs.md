## Meaning and Definition
Which List contains "Forests" and "Protection of wild animals and birds" today?

Explanation. Both subjects were moved to the Concurrent List (Entries 17A and 17B) by the 42nd Amendment, 1976.

Forests and wildlife protection were moved to the Concurrent List by which constitutional amendment?

Explanation. The Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976 moved Forests and Wildlife protection to the Concurrent List.

"Water" is listed in which List of the Seventh Schedule?

Explanation. "Water" is Entry 17 of the State List, ordinarily making it a State subject.
