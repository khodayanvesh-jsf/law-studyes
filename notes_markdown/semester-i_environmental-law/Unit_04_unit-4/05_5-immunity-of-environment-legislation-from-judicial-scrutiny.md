# 5. immunity of Environment legislation from judicial scrutiny (Art.31C)
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 4:** Unit 4
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/immunity-of-environment-legislation-from-judicial-scrutiny/

## In One Minute
Article 31C protects certain laws from being struck down for breaching Article 14 (equality) or Article 19 (the six freedoms). The law must give effect to the State policy in Article 39(b) or (c). Those clauses deal with sharing the community's material resources and preventing concentration of wealth. The 42nd Amendment tried to extend the shield to every Directive Principle. The Supreme Court struck that extension down in Minerva Mills (1980). Article 31C does not protect a law from a challenge under Article 21.

## Legal Provision
- Article 31C: as printed, a law giving effect to State policy on the principles in Part IV is not void for inconsistent with Article 14 or Article 19. This applies notwithstanding Article 13. Since Minerva Mills, only the principles in Article 39(b) and (c) count. State laws: a State law gets this protection only if it was reserved for the President and received his assent. Article 39(b): the ownership and control of the material resources of the community are so distributed as best to serve the common good. Article 39(c): the economic system must not concentrate wealth and the means of production to the common detriment. History in the footnotes: inserted by the 25th Amendment, 1971 (in force 20 April 1972). The 42nd Amendment, section 4, widened it to Part IV as a whole (in force 3 January 1977). Section 4 was declared invalid in Minerva Mills.

## Meaning and Definition
Article 31C is a shield , not a power. It does not let the State make a law. It protects a law that is already made from a particular kind of attack. The protected laws are those that give effect to the State policy in Article 39(b) or (c).

The shield covers only two attacks: Article 14 and Article 19. A law protected by Article 31C can still be challenged under Article 21.

Does Article 31C shield this law? How to read this: start at the top. Each box must be answered "yes" to move down. The text beside a box shows where the shield fails. 1. Does the law give effect to the policy in Article 39(b) or Article 39(c)? Minerva Mills No: Article 31C does not apply. 2. If it is a State law, was it reserved for the President and did he assent? Proviso to Article 31C No: Article 31C does not apply to it. 3. Is the challenge under Article 14 or Article 19? Article 31C No: a challenge under Article 21 is not shielded. 4. Result The law cannot be struck down on that ground.

## Key Points to Remember
- Article 31C shields from Articles 14 and 19, not Article 21. Only laws giving effect to Article 39(b) or (c) qualify. 25th Amendment, 1971; widened by the 42nd Amendment, 1976. Minerva Mills (1980): extension struck down, 4:1. A State law needs the President's assent.
