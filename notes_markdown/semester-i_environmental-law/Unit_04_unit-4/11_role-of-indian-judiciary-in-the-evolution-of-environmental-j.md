## Meaning and Definition
Which case held that the right to life under Article 21 includes the right to enjoy pollution-free water and air?

Explanation. In Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar (1991), the Court held that life includes the right to enjoy pollution-free water and air. In Rural Litigation and Entitlement Kendra (1985), it had earlier read the right to a healthy environment into Article 21.

What was the actual outcome of the writ petition in Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar itself?

Explanation. The petition itself was dismissed because the Court found it driven by a private commercial dispute rather than genuine public interest.
