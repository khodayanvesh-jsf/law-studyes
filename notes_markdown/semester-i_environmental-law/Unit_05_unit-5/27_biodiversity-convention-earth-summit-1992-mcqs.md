## Meaning and Definition
The Convention on Biological Diversity was opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit on:

Explanation. The text was adopted at Nairobi on 22 May 1992 and opened for signature at Rio de Janeiro on 5 June 1992. It came into force on 29 December 1993.

Which two binding treaties were opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit?

Explanation. The UNFCCC and the CBD were both opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992.

The Convention on Biological Diversity's three objectives are:

Explanation. Article 1 of the CBD sets out three equally-ranked objectives: conservation, sustainable use, and fair and equitable benefit-sharing.
