## Meaning and Definition
Why does classical international law create a structural gap for multinational corporation (MNC) liability?

Explanation. International law in its classical form binds States, not private companies, so a victim usually cannot sue an MNC before an international tribunal the way one State can arbitrate against another.

In the Bhopal Gas Tragedy litigation, why did the US court dismiss the case against Union Carbide Corporation?

Explanation. The US District Court held India was the appropriate forum since the plant, victims, and evidence were all located there, sending the case back to Indian courts.

Which Indian legislation authorised the Government of India to represent all Bhopal victims in a single consolidated claim?

Explanation. The Bhopal Gas Leak Disaster (Processing of Claims) Act, 1985 authorised the Government of India to represent all victims in a consolidated claim, leading to the 1989 settlement.
