# 14. UN Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCC)

## In One Minute
The UN Framework Convention on Climate Change is the parent treaty of the climate regime. It was adopted on 9 May 1992, opened for signature at Rio and came into force on 21 March 1994. It sets an aim but no binding numbers. The Kyoto Protocol and the Paris Agreement were adopted under it. They did not replace it. Exam answers must keep that straight, and must know the three groups of Parties: Annex I, Annex II and non-Annex I.

## Legal Provision
- Adoption and entry into force: adopted at United Nations Headquarters, New York, on 9 May 1992; opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit; in force from 21 March 1994. India: signed on 10 June 1992 and ratified on 1 November 1993 (UN Treaty Collection). Article 2, objective: to stabilise greenhouse gas concentrations at a level that prevents dangerous human interference with the climate system. Article 3, principles: including common but differentiated responsibilities, with developed countries taking the lead, and precaution. Article 4, commitments: Annex II Parties are to provide new and additional financial resources, and technology transfer, so that developing countries can meet their commitments.

## Meaning and Definition
A framework convention sets up an aim, principles and institutions, and leaves detailed duties to later protocols. The UN Framework Convention on Climate Change is the framework for the world's response to climate change .

Its Parties meet at the Conference of the Parties , the Convention's supreme body. Kyoto was adopted at the third meeting in 1997 and Paris at the twenty-first in 2015.

## Key Points to Remember
- Adopted 9 May 1992; in force 21 March 1994. India ratified on 1 November 1993. Article 2: stabilise greenhouse gas concentrations; no binding numbers. Article 3: common but differentiated responsibilities and precaution. Annex II sits inside Annex I; India is non-Annex I. Kyoto (COP3) and Paris (COP21) were adopted under the Convention. Art 4(1): all Parties; Art 4(2): Annex I, aim of 1990 levels; Art 4(3)-(5): Annex II finance and technology; Art 4(7): developing countries depend on that support. Art 11: financial mechanism. Art 17: protocols. Art 23: in force on the 90th day after the 50th instrument. Art 24: no reservations.
