# 12. Kyoto Protocol 1997
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/kyoto-protocol-1997/

## In One Minute
The Kyoto Protocol was adopted on 11 December 1997. It gave binding numerical targets for cutting greenhouse gases to Annex I countries only, the developed and transition economies. Developing countries such as India had no binding target. It created three flexible market mechanisms, including the Clean Development Mechanism. It came into force on 16 February 2005 and was later extended by the Doha Amendment.

## Legal Provision
- Kyoto Protocol to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change: adopted at the third Conference of the Parties in Kyoto, Japan, on 11 December 1997. Entry into force: 16 February 2005, after ratification by at least 55 Parties covering at least 55 per cent of Annex I carbon dioxide emissions in 1990. Doha Amendment: adopted in December 2012 for a second commitment period from 2013 to 2020 with a target of 18 per cent below 1990 levels. It entered into force on 31 December 2020. India: a Party since accession on 26 August 2002, as a non-Annex I country. India accepted the Doha Amendment on 8 August 2017 (UN Treaty Collection).

## Meaning and Definition
The Kyoto Protocol is a treaty adopted under the Climate Change Convention. The Convention only stated a goal . The Protocol added numbers, deadlines and consequences. It applies the principle of common but differentiated responsibilities : all share the duty, but developed countries carry the first and heaviest burden.

## Key Points to Remember
- Adopted 11 December 1997; in force 16 February 2005. Binding targets only for Annex I countries. Mechanisms: emissions trading, Joint Implementation, Clean Development Mechanism. Doha Amendment: second period 2013 to 2020, in force 31 December 2020. India acceded on 26 August 2002 and had no binding target. It accepted the Doha Amendment on 8 August 2017. Art 3(1): at least 5 per cent below 1990 levels, 2008 to 2012. Art 3(7): Annex B percentage multiplied by five. Art 6: Joint Implementation. Art 12: Clean Development Mechanism. Art 17: emissions trading. Art 10: no new commitments for non-Annex I Parties. Art 25: 55 Parties and 55 per cent of Annex I carbon dioxide emissions for 1990.
