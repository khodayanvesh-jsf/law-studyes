# 7. The role of UNEP for the protection of environment
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/role-of-unep-for-the-protection-of-environment/

## In One Minute
UNEP is the United Nations Environment Programme. The General Assembly set it up on 15 December 1972, after the Stockholm conference. Its home is Nairobi. It is not an environmental police force. Its job is to watch the state of the environment, set the agenda and help States negotiate treaties. It cannot force any State to obey.

## Legal Provision
- General Assembly resolution 2997 (XXVII), 15 December 1972: the resolution on institutional and financial arrangements for international environmental cooperation, which led to UNEP and a 58-member Governing Council. General Assembly resolution 67/251 (March 2013): renamed the Governing Council as the UN Environment Assembly, after the Council moved to universal membership in 2013.

## Meaning and Definition
UNEP is the main UN body for environmental matters. It is a programme of the United Nations, not a treaty body. Its work is to coordinate, to advise and to build agreement among States.

## Key Points to Remember
- UNEP: General Assembly resolution 2997 (XXVII), 15 December 1972; Nairobi. Governing Council of 58 members, replaced by the UN Environment Assembly with universal membership. Role: monitor, set the agenda, support treaty-making. No enforcement power.
