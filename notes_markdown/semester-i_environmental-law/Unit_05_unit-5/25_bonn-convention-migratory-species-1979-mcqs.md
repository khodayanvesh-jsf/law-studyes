## Meaning and Definition
The Bonn Convention's correct, full title is:

Explanation. The Bonn Convention's correct title covers all migratory wild animals, not birds alone.

When was the Bonn Convention signed, and when did it enter into force?

Explanation. The Bonn Convention was signed on 23 June 1979 and entered into force on 1 November 1983 — well before 1992.

Under the Bonn Convention, species listed in Appendix I are subject to:

Explanation. Appendix I species are endangered migratory species requiring strict protection and a general prohibition on taking, subject to narrow exceptions.
