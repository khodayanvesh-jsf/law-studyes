## Meaning and Definition
UNEP was created by which instrument, and when?

Explanation. UNEP was established by UN General Assembly Resolution 2997 (XXVII) on 15 December 1972, following the Stockholm Conference's recommendation.

UNEP's headquarters are located in:

Explanation. UNEP is headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya.

UNEP's primary role in the international environmental regime is best described as:

Explanation. UNEP does not have direct enforcement power; its role is catalytic and coordinating, supporting the negotiation and administration of multilateral environmental agreements.
