## Meaning and Definition
What actually took place in Nairobi in 1982?

Explanation. There is no binding 'Nairobi Convention'; the 1982 Nairobi session was a UNEP Governing Council Special Session that adopted the non-binding Nairobi Declaration.

What did the Nairobi Declaration conclude about environmental progress since Stockholm (1972)?

Explanation. Reports of the session say the Declaration was positive about progress since 1972 but anxious about the future, and called for fresh commitment to environmental protection.

The Nairobi session's recommendation for a special commission directly led to the creation of:

Explanation. UN General Assembly Resolution 38/161 (1983) established the World Commission on Environment and Development, chaired by Gro Harlem Brundtland.
