## Meaning and Definition
Winfield defines tortious liability as arising from the breach of a duty primarily fixed by:

Explanation. Winfield's definition: tortious liability arises from the breach of a duty primarily fixed by law, owed towards persons generally.

Under Salmond's definition, the remedy for a tort is a common law action for:

Explanation. Salmond defines tort as a civil wrong remedied by unliquidated damages, not exclusively breach of contract or trust.

Which statute gives the statutory (negative) definition of "tort" in India?

Explanation. Section 2(m) of the Limitation Act, 1963 defines tort as a civil wrong which is not exclusively a breach of contract or trust.
