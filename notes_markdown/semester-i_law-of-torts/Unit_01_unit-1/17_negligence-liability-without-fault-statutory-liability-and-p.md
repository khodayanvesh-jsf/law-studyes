## Meaning and Definition
Negligence in tort law is best defined as:

Explanation. Negligence is the breach of a legal duty to take care, resulting in damage to the person to whom the duty was owed.

The rule of strict liability originates from which case?

Explanation. Strict liability is the rule from Rylands v. Fletcher — liability for a dangerous thing escaping from one's land, even without negligence.

Absolute liability, as laid down in M.C. Mehta v. Union of India (1987), differs from strict liability because it:

Explanation. Absolute liability applies to hazardous industries and admits none of strict liability's traditional defences.
