## Meaning and Definition
What does the principle of restitutio in integrum mean in the context of assessing damages?

Explanation. Restitutio in integrum is the general principle that damages should, as far as money can achieve it, restore the plaintiff to the position they would have occupied had the tort not been committed.

What are 'exemplary' or 'punitive' damages intended to do?

Explanation. Exemplary (punitive) damages go beyond compensating the plaintiff — they are awarded to punish and deter particularly egregious, malicious, or oppressive conduct by the defendant.

What is the 'once and for all' rule regarding assessment of damages?

Explanation. The 'once and for all' rule requires that damages be assessed in a single lump sum at the time of judgment, covering both past and anticipated future loss, without any later reopening of the assessment.
