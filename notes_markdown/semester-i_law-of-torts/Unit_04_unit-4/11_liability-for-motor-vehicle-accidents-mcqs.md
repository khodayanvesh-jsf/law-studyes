## Meaning and Definition
Under ordinary tort principles, liability for a motor vehicle accident is generally based on:

Explanation. At its foundation, motor vehicle accident liability is liability in negligence, requiring proof of a duty of care, its breach, and resulting damage.

Section 164 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 provides:

Explanation. Section 164 provides no-fault liability — fixed compensation for death or grievous hurt, without proof of any wrongful act, neglect or default. It replaced Sections 140 to 144, omitted from 1 April 2022.

Compensation payable under Section 164 is best described as:

Explanation. Section 164 compensation is a fixed amount, 5 lakh rupees for death and 2.5 lakh rupees for grievous hurt, payable without proving negligence.
