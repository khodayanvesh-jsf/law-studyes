## Meaning and Definition
What does the action for 'loss of consortium' historically compensate?

Explanation. Loss of consortium compensates a spouse for the loss of the other spouse's companionship, society, and services, where a third party's tort injures that other spouse.

In Best v Samuel Fox & Co Ltd (1952), why was the wife's claim for loss of consortium rejected?

Explanation. The House of Lords held that at common law, the action for loss of consortium was historically available only to a husband for injury to his wife, not vice versa — illustrating the doctrine's outdated, gender-asymmetric origins.

What is 'enticement' as a tort affecting family relations?

Explanation. Enticement refers to inducing one spouse to leave the other, historically recognised as a tort affecting the marital relationship.
