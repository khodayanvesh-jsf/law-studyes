## Meaning and Definition
What is the primary interest protected by the tort of injurious falsehood, as distinct from defamation?

Explanation. Injurious falsehood (trade libel/malicious falsehood) protects a plaintiff's economic or trade interests from false statements, unlike defamation, which protects personal reputation.

What did Ratcliffe v Evans (1892) establish regarding proof of damage in injurious falsehood?

Explanation. Bowen LJ held that where a false statement injures a class of business generally, general evidence of a decline in trade suffices — the plaintiff need not identify specific customers lost.

What are the essentials of the tort of injurious falsehood?

Explanation. Injurious falsehood requires a false statement made with malice, causing special (pecuniary) damage to the plaintiff's trade, business, or property interests.
