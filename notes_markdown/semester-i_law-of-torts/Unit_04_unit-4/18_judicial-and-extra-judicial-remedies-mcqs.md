## Meaning and Definition
How are remedies in the law of torts broadly divided?

Explanation. Remedies in tort are broadly divided into judicial remedies, which require court intervention, and extra-judicial (self-help) remedies, which can be exercised without going to court.

Which of the following is a judicial remedy in tort law?

Explanation. Damages, awarded through court proceedings, is a judicial remedy — the other three options are all extra-judicial (self-help) remedies.

What is 'expulsion of trespasser' as an extra-judicial remedy?

Explanation. Expulsion of trespasser allows a lawful occupier to use reasonable force, without breach of the peace, to remove a trespasser from their land, without first obtaining a court order.
