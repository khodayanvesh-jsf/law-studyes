## Meaning and Definition
Trespass to land is actionable:

Explanation. Trespass to land is actionable per se — the unauthorised interference with possession is itself the wrong, regardless of whether any measurable damage results.

In Entick v. Carrington (1765), the King's messengers' entry into the claimant's house was held unlawful because:

Explanation. No positive legal authority justified the entry — the absence of a specific prohibition was not treated as a substitute for actual authorisation.

What principle does Entick v. Carrington establish about government power?

Explanation. The case established that state power to trespass is no broader than any private person's, absent clear legal authorisation.
