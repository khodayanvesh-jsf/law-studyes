## Meaning and Definition
Which of the following is NOT one of the five essential elements of malicious prosecution?

Explanation. The five elements are: prosecution by the defendant, favourable termination, absence of reasonable and probable cause, malice, and damage — a separate perjury conviction is not required.

Does an acquittal, by itself, prove that the defendant lacked reasonable and probable cause?

Explanation. Reasonable and probable cause is judged at the time the prosecution began; an acquittal on later evidence does not automatically mean the original belief in guilt was unreasonable.

"Malice" in the context of malicious prosecution means:

Explanation. Malice here means an improper purpose behind the prosecution — not necessarily personal hatred, but any motive other than genuinely seeking justice.
