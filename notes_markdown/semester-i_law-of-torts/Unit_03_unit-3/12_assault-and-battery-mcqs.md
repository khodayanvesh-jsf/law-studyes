## Meaning and Definition
In Stephens v. Myers (1830), the defendant was liable for assault even though he:

Explanation. The defendant advanced with a clenched fist but was stopped before making contact — his liability rested on the apprehension of imminent contact created, not on any actual touching.

According to Cole v. Turner (1704), what degree of force is required for battery?

Explanation. Cole v. Turner established that "the least touching of another in anger is a battery" — the degree of force is irrelevant so long as it is unwanted and intentional.

What is the key difference between assault and battery?

Explanation. Assault protects against the apprehension of imminent unlawful contact; battery is the actual unwanted physical contact itself.
