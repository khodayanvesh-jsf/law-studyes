## Meaning and Definition
Under which section of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 is the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) established?

Explanation. Section 10 empowers the Central Government to establish the CCPA by notification.

What is the key difference between the CCPA and the District/State/National Commissions?

Explanation. The CCPA's mandate is regulatory/preventive protection of consumers as a class, distinct from the Commissions' role of adjudicating individual complaints.

On what basis can the CCPA initiate an inquiry or investigation?

Explanation. The CCPA can act suo motu (on its own initiative), on a complaint, or on the direction of the Central Government.
