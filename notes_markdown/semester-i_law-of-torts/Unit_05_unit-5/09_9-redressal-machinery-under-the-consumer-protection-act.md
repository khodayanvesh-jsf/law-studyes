# 9. Redressal Machinery under the Consumer Protection Act
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/redressal-machinery-under-the-consumer-protection-act/

## In One Minute
The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 sets up a three-tier machinery for consumer disputes: the District Commission in each district, the State Commission in each State and the National Commission. It adds mediation cells and a regulator, the Central Consumer Protection Authority. In exams, this topic is asked as "explain the redressal machinery under the Act", and as a problem on where to file and how to appeal. The usual trap is to mix the composition, the value limits and the appeal periods of the three tiers.

## Legal Provision
- Section 28: a District Commission in each district, with a President and not less than two members. Section 42: a State Commission in each State, with a President and not less than four members. Section 53 and 54: the National Commission, with a President and not less than four members. Sections 34, 47 and 58: jurisdiction of the three Commissions. Section 38: procedure and powers of a Commission, including interim orders and the powers of a civil court. Sections 40, 50 and 60: review of orders. Sections 41, 51 and 67: appeals. Sections 71 and 72: enforcement of orders and penalty for non-compliance. Sections 74 to 81: mediation. Sections 34(2) and 47(4): where a complaint is filed with the District and State Commissions (territorial jurisdiction). Section 35: who may file a complaint, and the fee. Section 36: admission of a complaint within 21 days, and how the bench sits. Section 39: the reliefs a Commission may order. Sections 52 and 73: time to decide an appeal, and appeals against orders under section 72.

## Meaning and Definition
The redressal machinery means the set of agencies that the Act creates to hear and decide consumer disputes. A "consumer dispute" is a dispute in which the person against whom a complaint is made denies or disputes the allegations (section 2(8)).

## Key Points to Remember
- Three tiers: District (sections 28, 34), State (sections 42, 47), National (sections 53, 58). Members: President and at least two (District), at least four (State and National). Appeals: 45 days to the State Commission, then 30 days each, with 50 per cent deposit. Commissions have civil court powers and can pass interim orders. Mediation cells and the Authority add to the machinery. Time limits: complaint two years (section 69); appeals 45, 30 and 30 days; review 30 days; late filing allowed for sufficient cause.
