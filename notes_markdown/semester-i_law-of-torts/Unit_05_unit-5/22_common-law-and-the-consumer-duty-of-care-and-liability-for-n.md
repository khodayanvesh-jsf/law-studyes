## Meaning and Definition
Before any consumer-specific statute existed, which tort was the primary remedy available to a consumer injured by a defective product or careless service?

Explanation. Before consumer-specific legislation, an injured consumer's only real remedy was the tort of negligence — proving a duty of care, breach, and resulting damage.

What barrier did the doctrine of privity of contract traditionally create for an injured consumer?

Explanation. Privity of contract meant a buyer generally had no claim against a manufacturer or other party they had no direct contract with, even if that party's carelessness caused the harm.

In Donoghue v. Stevenson, what did the House of Lords hold about a manufacturer's duty of care?

Explanation. The House of Lords held that a manufacturer's duty of care to the ultimate consumer exists independently of contract, removing privity as a bar to a negligence claim.
