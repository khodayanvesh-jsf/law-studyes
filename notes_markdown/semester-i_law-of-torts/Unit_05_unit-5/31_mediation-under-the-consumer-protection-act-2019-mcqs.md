## Meaning and Definition
Which chapter of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 introduces the formal mediation mechanism?

Explanation. Chapter V, Sections 74 to 81, introduces the formal statutory mediation mechanism under the 2019 Act.

Under Section 74, where are consumer mediation cells attached?

Explanation. Section 74 requires mediation cells attached to District Commissions, State Commissions, the National Commission, and its regional Benches.

How are mediators selected under Sections 75-76?

Explanation. Mediators are empanelled by the Commission, and for each dispute referred to mediation, one is nominated from that empanelled panel.
