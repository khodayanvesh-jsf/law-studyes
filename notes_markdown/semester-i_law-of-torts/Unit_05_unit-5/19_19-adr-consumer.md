# 19. ADR & consumer
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/adr-and-consumer/

## In One Minute
Alternative dispute resolution (ADR) means settling a dispute without a full trial. The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 builds one ADR method into the Commissions: mediation. A Commission can send the parties to mediation if a settlement looks possible, and the parties must agree in writing. In exams, this topic is asked as a short note on ADR and consumer disputes, or on mediation under the Act. The usual trap is to think that mediation is compulsory. It needs the written consent of both parties.

## Legal Provision
- Section 37: at the first hearing or later, if elements of a settlement acceptable to the parties appear, the District Commission may ask the parties to give written consent within five days to mediation under Chapter V. On consent, it refers the matter within five days. prescribed cases are excluded. Section 74: a consumer mediation cell is set up with each District and State Commission by the State Government, and with the National Commission and its regional Benches by the Central Government. Section 75: each Commission prepares a panel of mediators on the recommendation of a selection committee of its President and a member. The panel is valid for five years. Section 79: mediation is held in the cell, and the mediator is guided by the principles of natural justice. Section 80: a settlement is written down and signed by the parties, and the mediator sends it with a report to the Commission. Section 81: the Commission passes an order recording the settlement within seven days. Section 100: the Act is in addition to, and not in derogation of, any other law in force. ## Meaning and Definition
ADR covers methods such as negotiation, mediation, conciliation, arbitration and Lok Adalats. In these methods, the parties, with the help of a neutral person or body, reach a result outside the ordinary court process. In the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the method given a place inside the Act is mediation. A mediator helps the parties reach their own agreement and does not decide the dispute.

## Key Points to Remember
- Mediation needs written consent of the parties within five days (section 37). A consumer mediation cell is attached to each Commission (section 74). The panel of mediators is valid for five years (section 75). The Commission records a settlement within seven days (section 81). If mediation fails, the Commission hears all issues. Mediation ends in three months from the first appearance unless extended (Regulation 11(2)).
