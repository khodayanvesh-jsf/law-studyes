# 10. Consumer Protection Councils
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/consumer-protection-councils/

## In One Minute
The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 creates three advisory bodies: the Central Council, a State Council in each State and a District Council in each district. They advise on promotion and protection of consumer rights. They do not decide disputes. In exams, this topic is asked as "explain the composition and objects of the Consumer Protection Councils". The usual trap is to confuse the Councils, which advise, with the Commissions, which decide.

## Legal Provision
- Sections 3 to 5: the Central Consumer Protection Council, its meetings and objects. Sections 6 and 7: State Consumer Protection Councils and their objects. Sections 8 and 9: District Consumer Protection Councils and their objects. Section 2(9): the consumer rights that the Councils are to promote and protect. Corresponding sections of the 1986 Act: sections 4 to 8B.

## Meaning and Definition
A Consumer Protection Council is an advisory body. Its purpose is to render advice on the promotion and protection of consumer rights under the Act. It has no power to hear complaints or pass orders.

## Key Points to Remember
- Councils are advisory and do not decide disputes. Chairs: Union Minister (Central), State Minister (State), Collector (District). Meetings: at least one a year (Central), at least two a year (State, District). Object: advice on promotion and protection of consumer rights. Sections 3 to 9 of the 2019 Act.
