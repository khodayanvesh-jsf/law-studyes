## Meaning and Definition
Under section 34(1) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, the District Commission hears complaints where the consideration paid does not exceed:

Explanation. Section 34(1) fixes the limit at Rs 1 crore and lets the Central Government prescribe another value. The 2021 Rules prescribed Rs 50 lakh. Section 107 repealed the Consumer Protection Act, 1986.

What is the single most significant structural innovation of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019?

Explanation. The CCPA is a dedicated regulatory body that can investigate and act on its own initiative, without waiting for an individual consumer complaint — something the 1986 Act had no equivalent of.

How does the 2019 Act treat product liability differently from the position under the 1986 Act?

Explanation. The 2019 Act, for the first time, gives product liability its own dedicated statutory chapter with defined standards, instead of requiring reliance purely on common-law negligence.
