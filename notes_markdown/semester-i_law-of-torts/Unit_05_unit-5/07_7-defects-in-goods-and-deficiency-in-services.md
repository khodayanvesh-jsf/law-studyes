# 7. Defects in goods and deficiency in services
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/defects-in-goods-and-deficiency-in-services/

## In One Minute
A defect is a fault in goods. A deficiency is a fault in a service. Both are grounds for a consumer complaint. Deficiency includes negligence and the deliberate withholding of relevant information. In exams, this topic is asked as "distinguish defect from deficiency and explain the remedies", and as a problem on a faulty product or a bad service. The trap is to forget that goods are tested in a laboratory and services are judged on the evidence.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(10): defines "defect". Section 2(11): defines "deficiency". Section 2(42): defines "service". It excludes services rendered free of charge or under a contract of personal service. Section 2(6): a complaint may allege that goods suffer from defects, that services suffer from deficiency, that a price above the fixed or displayed price was charged, or that hazardous goods or services are offered. Section 38(2): procedure when goods are alleged to be defective, including laboratory testing. Section 39: findings and reliefs of the District Commission.

## Meaning and Definition
A defect is any fault, imperfection or shortcoming in the quality, quantity, potency , purity or standard in relation to the goods. The standard is one required to be maintained by or under any law or under any contract, express or implied . It also covers a standard claimed by the trader in any manner.

A deficiency is any fault, imperfection, shortcoming or inadequacy in the quality, nature and manner of performance of a service. The performance is one required to be maintained by or under any law, or one that a person has undertaken to perform under a contract or otherwise. It includes any act of negligence or omission or commission by that person that causes loss or injury to the consumer, and deliberate withholding of relevant information from the consumer.

## Key Points to Remember
- Defect: goods. Deficiency: services. Standard comes from law, contract or the trader's own claim. Deficiency includes negligence and withholding of relevant information. Laboratory report within 45 days. Free services and personal service contracts are outside the Act.
