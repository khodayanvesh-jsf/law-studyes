## Meaning and Definition
What best describes 'consumerism'?

Explanation. Consumerism is a collective, organised movement — through associations, advocacy, and legislative lobbying — not merely individual shopping caution.

Why couldn't ordinary tort remedies alone correct market-wide consumer harms such as adulteration or false advertising?

Explanation. Tort remedies compensate one injured consumer at a time, long after harm occurs, and only if that consumer can afford to litigate — inadequate for correcting market-wide problems.

The doctrine of caveat emptor means

Explanation. Caveat emptor, 'let the buyer beware', historically placed the responsibility of examining goods before purchase on the buyer, with limited seller disclosure duties.
