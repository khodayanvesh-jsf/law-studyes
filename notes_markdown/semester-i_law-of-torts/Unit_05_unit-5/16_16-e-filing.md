# 16. E-filing
**Subject:** Law of Torts (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/law-of-torts/e-filing/

## In One Minute
The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 allows a consumer complaint to be filed electronically, the fee to be paid electronically, the hearing to be held by video conferencing, and notices to be served by electronic means. This saves travel and cost, and it helps a consumer file where he lives. In exams, this topic is asked as a short note on e-filing and other electronic features of the 2019 Act. The usual trap is to confuse e-filing, which is a procedure, with e-commerce, which is the market that the Act also regulates.

## Legal Provision
- Section 35(1), proviso: a complaint may be filed electronically in the manner prescribed . Section 35(2): the fee is payable in the manner prescribed, including in electronic form. Section 38(6): a party may be heard in person or through video conferencing , with leave of the Commission. Section 38(4): the Commission may require an electronic service provider to give information, documents or records . Section 65: notices may be served by registered post, speed post, an approved courier, or any other mode including electronic means. A notice may also be served on an electronic service provider at the address on its platform. Section 17: a complaint of violation of consumer rights as a class may be sent in writing or in electronic mode to the District Collector, a regional Commissioner or the Central Authority. Section 2(16) and 2(17): definitions of e-commerce and of electronic service provider. Section 94: the Central Government may take measures to prevent unfair trade practices in e-commerce and direct selling.

## Meaning and Definition
E-filing means making a complaint and other filings through an electronic system, instead of on paper at the registry. The Act does not itself describe the portal or the format. It leaves the manner to be prescribed by rules, and a government online system for consumer complaints has been provided for this purpose.

## Key Points to Remember
- Complaint may be filed electronically (section 35(1) proviso). Fee may be paid in electronic form (section 35(2)). Video conferencing hearing with leave (section 38(6)). Notices by electronic means (section 65). Limitation of two years still applies.
