## Meaning and Definition
Bioethics in environmental law mainly deals with:

Explanation. Bioethics studies the moral questions that arise from using living organisms, now including biodiversity, genes, animals and ecosystems.

Which ethical principle is closest to the precautionary principle?

Explanation. Non-maleficence (avoid avoidable harm) points the same way as precaution: do not delay protection merely for lack of full proof.

The view that nature has value mainly because it serves human needs is called:

Explanation. The anthropocentric view treats nature's value as arising from human benefit.
