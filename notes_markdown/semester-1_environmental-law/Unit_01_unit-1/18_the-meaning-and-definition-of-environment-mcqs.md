## Meaning and Definition
Under Section 2(a) of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, 'environment' includes water, air, land and the inter-relationship between them and:

Explanation. Section 2(a) expressly includes human beings, other living creatures, plants, micro-organisms, and property in its definition of environment, alongside water, air, and land.

Explanation. German biologist Ernst Haeckel coined the term 'ecology' in 1866, from the Greek 'oikos' (habitat) and 'logos' (study).

Which of the following is the correct order of ecological levels of organisation, from smallest to largest?

Explanation. The correct order is Organism (individual) → Population (same species in an area) → Community (all species in an area) → Ecosystem (community + physical environment).

In an ecosystem, which biotic component breaks down dead organic matter and recycles nutrients?

Explanation. Decomposers (fungi and bacteria) break down dead organic matter, recycling nutrients back into the ecosystem.
