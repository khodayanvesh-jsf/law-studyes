## Meaning and Definition
Climate justice is best described as:

Explanation. Climate justice treats climate change as a question of fairness and rights: who caused it, who suffers and who pays for the response.

Which dimension of climate justice is based on the idea that today's generation holds the planet in trust for future generations?

Explanation. Intergenerational justice rests on the duty to leave the environment in a fit state for future generations.

The principle that all States must act on climate change, but developed countries carry a larger share of the duty, is called:

Explanation. CBDR, written into the UNFCCC (1992) and carried into the Paris Agreement (2016), differentiates duties by past emissions and capacity.
