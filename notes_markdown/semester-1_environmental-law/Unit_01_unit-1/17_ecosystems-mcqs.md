## Meaning and Definition
Biodiversity is studied at which three levels?

Explanation. Biodiversity is studied at three levels: genetic diversity (within a species), species diversity (within an ecosystem), and ecosystem diversity (across a region).

The many traditional rice varieties grown across India, each suited to different soil and climate conditions, best illustrate:

Explanation. Different varieties within the same species (rice) suited to different conditions is an example of genetic diversity.

India has parts of how many globally recognised biodiversity hotspots?

Explanation. India has parts of 4 globally recognised biodiversity hotspots: the Himalayas, the Western Ghats, Indo-Burma, and Sundaland.
