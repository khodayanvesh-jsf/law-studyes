## In One Minute
Climate change is a long-term shift in temperature, rainfall and weather patterns. Global warming is its main driver, but climate change covers everything the warming sets off. The UN Framework Convention on Climate Change gives it a legal meaning. Exams ask for the meaning, the effects and the difference from global warming.

## Legal Provision
- Article 1(2), UN Framework Convention on Climate Change, 1992: "climate change" means a change of climate attributed directly or indirectly to human activity that alters the composition of the global atmosphere, in addition to natural climate variability observed over comparable periods. Section 3(1), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: the Central Government's power to take measures to protect the environment and control pollution.

## Meaning and Definition
Weather is the state of the air on a given day, such as rain or heat. Climate is the usual pattern of weather in a region over many years. Climate change is a long-term shift in that pattern.

The Convention separates climate change caused by human activity from natural variation. The shift caused by humans is the legal concern, because it can be reduced by law and policy.

## Key Points to Remember
- Climate change: long-term shift in weather patterns. UNFCCC, Article 1(2): change attributed to human activity, beyond natural variability. Global warming is the main driver; climate change is the wider result. Effects: extreme weather, melting ice, rising seas, species loss, food and health risks. Art 1 also defines adverse effects, climate system, emissions, source, sink and reservoir. Art 2: objective. Art 4(8): vulnerable countries.
