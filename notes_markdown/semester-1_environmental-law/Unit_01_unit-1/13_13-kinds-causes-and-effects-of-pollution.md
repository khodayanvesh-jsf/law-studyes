## In One Minute
Pollution comes in six main kinds: air, water, soil, noise, thermal and radioactive. Each has its own sources and its own harm. Most pollution today comes from human activity. Its effects fall on health, nature, the climate and the economy. This is one of the most common long questions in the paper.

## Legal Provision
- Section 2(b) and (c), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: an environmental pollutant is a substance in a concentration injurious to environment, and environmental pollution is its presence in the environment. Section 2(e), Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974: defines pollution of water, including change in its physical, chemical or biological properties. Section 2(a) and (b), Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981: defines an air pollutant, which includes noise, and air pollution. Section 3(1), Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: the Central Government's power to take measures to prevent, control and reduce pollution of every kind.

## Meaning and Definition
Pollution is the introduction of harmful substances or energy into the environment beyond what it can absorb . A kind of pollution is named by the part of the environment that is harmed or by the form of the pollutant.

## Key Points to Remember
- Six kinds: air, water, soil, noise, thermal, radioactive. Human causes dominate: industry, transport, farming, waste, mining, deforestation, population. Effects: health, nature, climate, economy.
