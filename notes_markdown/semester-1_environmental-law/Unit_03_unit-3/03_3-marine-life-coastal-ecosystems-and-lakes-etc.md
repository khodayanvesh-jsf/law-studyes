# 3. marine life, coastal ecosystems and lakes etc.

## In One Minute
No single Act protects the sea, the coast and lakes. Several laws apply. The Maritime Zones Act, 1976 fixes India's sea limits. The Water Act can cover sea and tidal waters if a State notifies them. A Coastal Regulation Zone notification controls building near the shore. Lakes and wetlands are protected by the Wetlands Rules, 2017. The Supreme Court has stopped shrimp farms in the coastal zone and applied the public trust doctrine to natural resources.

## Legal Provision
- Section 3, Territorial Waters, Continental Shelf, Exclusive Economic Zone and Other Maritime Zones Act, 1976: Indian sovereignty extends to the territorial waters, with their seabed and subsoil and the air above. The outer limit is twelve nautical miles from the nearest point of the baseline. The Central Government may alter the limit by notification. Section 2(j), Water Act, 1974: "stream" includes sea or tidal waters, to the extent or point the State Government specifies by notification. Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: the source of the Coastal Regulation Zone notifications, made under the Central Government's power to restrict areas and set safeguards. Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017: published in the Gazette on 26 September 2017. Article 48A and Article 51A(g): the State must protect the environment, and citizens must protect lakes, rivers and wild life.

## Meaning and Definition
This topic covers three habitats that face different pressures. Marine life lives in the sea and is exposed to discharge from land and ships. Coastal ecosystems include mangroves, coral reefs and the estuary where a river meets the sea. Lakes are inland water bodies, often within a wetland .

India also has an exclusive economic zone , where a coastal State has sovereign rights over resources and a duty to protect the marine environment. The 1976 Act is the Indian law on these zones.

## Key Points to Remember
- Territorial waters extend twelve nautical miles from the baseline (1976 Act, section 3). Water Act covers sea or tidal waters only as the State notifies (section 2(j)). CRZ is a notification under the 1986 Act, revised in 2019. Wetlands Rules, 2017: wise use, no conversion, no new industry. Kamal Nath: public trust. S. Jagannath: no shrimp farms in the prohibited coastal area.
