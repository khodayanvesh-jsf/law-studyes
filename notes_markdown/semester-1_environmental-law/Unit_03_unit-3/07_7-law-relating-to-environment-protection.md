# 7. Law relating to environment protection
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester 1) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/law-relating-to-environment-protection/

## In One Minute
The Water Act and the Air Act each deal with one medium. Three more laws deal with the environment in general: the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 and the hazardous waste rules made under the 1986 Act. This note shows how they fit together and which one answers which problem.

## Legal Provision
- Environment (Protection) Act, 1986: sections 3 (measures), 5 (directions), 6 (rules), 7 and 8 (standards and hazardous substances). Biological Diversity Act, 2002: section 3 (approval of the National Biodiversity Authority) and section 7 (prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board), 6 (intellectual property rights), 21 (benefit sharing). Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016: made under sections 6, 8 and 25 of the 1986 Act.

## Meaning and Definition
The "law relating to environment protection" here means the laws that cover the environment as a whole, not one medium. The 1986 Act is the base law. The 2002 Act deals with biological resources. The 2016 Rules deal with waste. The Rules come from the 1986 Act.
