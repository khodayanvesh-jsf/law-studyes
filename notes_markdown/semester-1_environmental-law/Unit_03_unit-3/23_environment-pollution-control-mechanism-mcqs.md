## Meaning and Definition
The National Green Tribunal hears civil cases involving a substantial environmental question under how many listed Acts?

Explanation. Section 14 gives the Tribunal jurisdiction over civil cases where a substantial environmental question arises from the Acts in Schedule I, which lists seven Acts.

The NGT's bench composition deliberately combines:

Explanation. The NGT combines Judicial Members with Expert Members qualified in environmental science/engineering, since environmental disputes routinely involve technical questions.

Which of the following is NOT listed in Schedule I of the NGT Act, and therefore outside the Tribunal's original jurisdiction for disputes arising purely under it?

Explanation. The Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 is NOT listed in Schedule I of the NGT Act — a frequently tested exclusion. Disputes purely under it go to the High Court or Supreme Court.
