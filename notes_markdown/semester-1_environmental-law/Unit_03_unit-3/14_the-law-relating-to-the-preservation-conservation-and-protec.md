## Meaning and Definition
The Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 has been officially renamed by the 2023 Amendment as:

Explanation. The Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act, 2023 renamed the parent Act as the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam. The Amendment received the President's assent on 4 August 2023.

Under Section 2 of the Van Adhiniyam, which of the following requires prior Central Government approval?

Explanation. Section 2 requires prior Central Government approval before forest land can be used for any non-forest purpose, among other restricted acts.

Which of the following is NOT one of the four acts restricted by Section 2 of the Van Adhiniyam?

Explanation. Section 2 requires prior Central Government approval for de-reservation, use for a non-forest purpose, assignment by lease, and clearing naturally grown trees for reafforestation. A survey without a change of land use is not one of these four orders.
