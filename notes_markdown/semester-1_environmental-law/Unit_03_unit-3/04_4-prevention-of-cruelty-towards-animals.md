# 4. Prevention of cruelty towards animals
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester 1) | **Unit 3:** Unit 3
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/prevention-of-cruelty-towards-animals/

## In One Minute
The Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 protects individual animals from unnecessary pain and suffering. Section 3 puts a duty of care on every person in charge of an animal. Section 11 lists acts of cruelty. In Animal Welfare Board of India v. A. Nagaraja the Supreme Court treated the Act as welfare law, held that jallikattu and bullock-cart races violate it, and read it with Article 51A(g). On 18 May 2023 a Constitution Bench upheld the Tamil Nadu amendment of 2017 that permits jallikattu, and disagreed with the 2014 decision (Animal Welfare Board of India v. Union of India).

## Legal Provision
- Article 51A(g): it is a fundamental duty of every citizen to have compassion for living creatures. Article 48: the State must organise agriculture and animal husbandry on modern and scientific lines. It must take steps to preserve and improve breeds and to prohibit the slaughter of cows and calves and other milch and draught cattle. Entry 17 of the Concurrent List: prevention of cruelty to animals. Parliament and the States can both make laws on it. Section 3: it is the duty of every person having the care or charge of an animal to take all reasonable measures to ensure its well-being. The person must prevent unnecessary pain or suffering. Section 4: the Central Government establishes the Animal Welfare Board of India. Section 11: treating animals cruelly. Sections 14, 15 and 17: experiments on animals and the Committee that controls and supervises them. Section 22: exhibiting and training performing animals.

## Meaning and Definition
The Act defines "animal" as any living creature other than a human being (section 2(a)). It protects the welfare of each animal. The wild life law protects species and habitats. This Act deals with the suffering of the individual animal, domestic or captive.

Main parts of the Act How to read this: start at the top box and follow the lines down. Each box is a part of the Act with its section. Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960 Duty of care (section 3) Reasonable measures for the animal's well-being. Animal Welfare Board (section 4) Promotes animal welfare and advises the Government. Cruelty (section 11) Listed acts, with fines and possible imprisonment. Experiments (sections 14, 15, 17) Allowed, but supervised by a Committee. Performing animals (section 22) Registration is required.

## Key Points to Remember
- Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960: unnecessary pain or suffering. Section 3: duty of care. Section 4: Animal Welfare Board. Section 11: cruelty. Sections 14, 15, 17: experiments and the supervising Committee. Section 22: performing animals. Article 51A(g): compassion. Article 48: cattle. Entry 17: Concurrent List. Nagaraja: jallikattu and bullock-cart races violate the Act.
