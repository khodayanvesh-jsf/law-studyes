Explanation. The NET Act gave no-fault liability and compensation for accidents while handling hazardous substances.

Explanation. Under the NET Act, appeal lay to the Supreme Court.

The National Environment Appellate Authority Act, 1997 dealt with:

Explanation. The NEAA heard appeals against environmental clearance for projects in restricted areas.
