## Meaning and Definition
What actually took place in Johannesburg in 2002?

Explanation. There is no binding 'Johannesburg Convention'; the actual event was the WSSD, producing non-binding outputs.

The Johannesburg Summit is commonly referred to as:

Explanation. The WSSD is commonly called 'Rio+10' because it marked ten years since the 1992 Rio Earth Summit.

The World Summit on Sustainable Development, Johannesburg, 26 August to 4 September 2002, was held under which General Assembly resolution?

Explanation. The Summit was held under General Assembly resolution 55/199 as a ten-year review of the Rio conference.
