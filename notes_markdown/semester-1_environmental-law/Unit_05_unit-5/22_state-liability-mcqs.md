## Meaning and Definition
Under general international law, State responsibility requires which two elements?

Explanation. State responsibility arises when conduct is attributable to the State under international law and that conduct breaches an international obligation.

In Trail Smelter, why was Canada held responsible even though the smelter was privately owned?

Explanation. A State is responsible for permitting or failing to prevent a harmful activity within its territory, even where the operator is a private entity.

Modern international law treats a State's duty to prevent transboundary environmental harm as an obligation of:

Explanation. The dominant view treats this as a due-diligence obligation, not automatic strict liability.
