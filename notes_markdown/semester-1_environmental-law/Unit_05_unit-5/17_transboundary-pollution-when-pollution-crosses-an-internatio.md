## Meaning and Definition
Transboundary pollution is best defined as pollution that:

Explanation. Transboundary pollution originates in one State's territory or control and causes or threatens environmental harm in another State or in areas beyond any State's jurisdiction.

Which of these is an example of 'diffuse or global' transboundary harm rather than 'point-to-point' harm?

Explanation. Greenhouse gases mix into the shared global atmosphere before causing harm, so no single source can be traced to a specific injury in a specific country — this is diffuse/global transboundary harm.

The 'no-harm' principle in international environmental law was first authoritatively established by:

Explanation. The Trail Smelter Arbitration between the United States and Canada first held that a State must not allow its territory to be used to cause serious, provable injury to another State's territory.
