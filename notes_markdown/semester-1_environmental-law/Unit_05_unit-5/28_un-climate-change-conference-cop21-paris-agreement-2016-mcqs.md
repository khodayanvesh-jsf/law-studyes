## Meaning and Definition
The Paris Agreement was adopted at which Conference of the Parties, and when?

Explanation. The Paris Agreement was adopted by consensus on 12 December 2015 at COP21 in Paris.

The Paris Agreement's core structural departure from the Kyoto Protocol is its use of:

Explanation. Paris replaced Kyoto's top-down Annex I-only targets with universal, self-determined NDCs from every party.

The Paris Agreement's temperature goal is to hold the global average temperature increase to:

Explanation. The Agreement aims to hold warming well below 2°C, with efforts to pursue 1.5°C.
