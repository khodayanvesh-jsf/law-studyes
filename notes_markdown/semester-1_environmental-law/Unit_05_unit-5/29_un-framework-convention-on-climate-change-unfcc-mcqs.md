## Meaning and Definition
The UNFCCC was opened for signature at which event, and when did it enter into force?

Explanation. The UNFCCC was opened for signature at the Rio Earth Summit in June 1992 and entered into force on 21 March 1994.

The UNFCCC's Article 2 objective is best described as:

Explanation. Article 2 sets a qualitative objective without binding numerical targets — those came later through Kyoto and Paris.

Which principle, found in UNFCCC Article 3, states that developed countries should take the lead in combating climate change?

Explanation. CBDR-RC recognises developed countries' greater historical contribution to emissions and greater capacity to act.
