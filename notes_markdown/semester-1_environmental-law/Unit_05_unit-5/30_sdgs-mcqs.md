## Meaning and Definition
The Sustainable Development Goals were adopted by the UN General Assembly in:

Explanation. The SDGs were adopted in September 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda.

How many SDGs and targets are there?

Explanation. There are 17 goals and 169 targets.

The Sustainable Development Goals were adopted by which General Assembly resolution?

Explanation. General Assembly resolution 70/1, "Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development", was adopted on 25 September 2015 without a vote.
