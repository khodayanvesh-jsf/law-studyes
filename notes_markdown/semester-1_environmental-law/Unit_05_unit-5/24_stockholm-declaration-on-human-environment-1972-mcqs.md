## Meaning and Definition
Where and when was the UN Conference on the Human Environment held?

Explanation. The UN Conference on the Human Environment was held in Stockholm, Sweden, from 5 to 16 June 1972.

The Stockholm Declaration is best described as:

Explanation. The Stockholm Declaration is non-binding soft law consisting of a Preamble and 26 Principles, not a treaty.

Which institution was directly created as a result of the Stockholm Conference?

Explanation. UNEP was created by UN General Assembly Resolution 2997 (XXVII) of 15 December 1972, following the Stockholm Conference's recommendation.
