## Meaning and Definition
The Kyoto Protocol was adopted at which Conference of the Parties, and when?

Explanation. The Kyoto Protocol was adopted on 11 December 1997 at the UNFCCC's Third Conference of the Parties (COP3) in Kyoto, Japan.

The Kyoto Protocol's defining structural feature is that it:

Explanation. Kyoto applies 'common but differentiated responsibilities' in strong form — binding targets for Annex I countries only.

Under Kyoto's first commitment period (2008-2012), Annex I countries collectively committed to reducing emissions by approximately:

Explanation. In the first commitment period, 2008 to 2012, Annex I countries together aimed at about 5 per cent below 1990 levels, each with its own figure. The Doha Amendment set 18 per cent below 1990 levels for 2013 to 2020.
