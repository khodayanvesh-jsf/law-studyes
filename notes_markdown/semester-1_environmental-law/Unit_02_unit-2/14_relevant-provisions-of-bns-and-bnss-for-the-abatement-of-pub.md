## Meaning and Definition
Public nuisance is defined under which section of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023?

Explanation. Section 270 BNS (earlier section 268 IPC) defines public nuisance as an act or illegal omission causing common injury, danger or annoyance to the public or to people who live nearby.

Which BNS section punishes voluntarily fouling the water of a public spring or reservoir?

Explanation. Section 279 BNS (earlier section 277 IPC) punishes a person who deliberately fouls the water of a public spring or reservoir. The punishment is imprisonment up to six months, or fine up to Rs 5,000, or both.
