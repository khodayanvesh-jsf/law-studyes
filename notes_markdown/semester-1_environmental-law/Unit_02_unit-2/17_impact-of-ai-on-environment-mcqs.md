Which of the following is a direct environmental harm linked to AI infrastructure?

Explanation. Data centres consume large amounts of electricity and cooling water.

Which Indian rules place collection and recycling duties on producers of electronic products?

Explanation. The E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 apply extended producer responsibility.

Is there a separate environmental statute in India dedicated only to AI?

Explanation. India has no AI-specific environmental statute; the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 and related laws apply.
