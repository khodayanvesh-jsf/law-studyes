## Meaning and Definition
Noise standards in India are fixed by the Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000, made under:

Explanation. The 2000 Rules are made under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.

Under the 2000 Rules, the permissible residential area limit is:

Explanation. Residential areas: 55 dB(A) by day and 45 dB(A) by night.

A silence zone under the Rules is an area of not less than how many metres around hospitals, educational institutions and courts?

Explanation. A silence zone is an area of not less than 100 metres around hospitals, educational institutions and courts.
