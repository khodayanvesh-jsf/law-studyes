## Meaning and Definition
The ENMOD Convention, 1976 prohibits:

Explanation. ENMOD bans hostile use of environmental modification techniques with widespread, long-lasting or severe effects.

Which provisions of Additional Protocol I, 1977 protect the natural environment in armed conflict?

Explanation. Articles 35(3) and 55 prohibit warfare causing widespread, long-term and severe damage to the natural environment.
