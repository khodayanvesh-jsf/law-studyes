## Meaning and Definition
Article 31C protects laws implementing which Directive Principles from an Article 14/19 challenge?

Explanation. Article 31C, in its current settled form, protects only laws genuinely implementing Article 39(b) or 39(c).

Which constitutional amendment first inserted Article 31C?

Explanation. Article 31C was first inserted by the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971.

A law qualifying for Article 31C's protection is shielded from challenge under which Articles?

Explanation. Article 31C shields a qualifying law only from challenge under Articles 14 and 19, not Article 21.
