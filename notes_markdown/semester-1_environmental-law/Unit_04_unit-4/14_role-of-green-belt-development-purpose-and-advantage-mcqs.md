## Meaning and Definition
A green belt differs from ordinary afforestation because:

Explanation. A green belt is a deliberately maintained zone tied to a specific regulatory or planning condition, unlike voluntary afforestation.

Which of the following is NOT a typical function of a green belt?

Explanation. A green belt has no function of increasing industrial output; its recognised functions are environmental and planning-related.

How is the size of the green belt around an industrial unit fixed?

Explanation. Each consent or clearance sets its own green-belt requirement, so a student should not quote a fixed percentage from memory.
