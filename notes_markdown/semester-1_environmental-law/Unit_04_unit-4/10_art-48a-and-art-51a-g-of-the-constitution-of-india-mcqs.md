## Meaning and Definition
Which Part of the Constitution contains Article 48A?

Explanation. Article 48A is a Directive Principle of State Policy, located in Part IV of the Constitution.

Article 51A(g) is found in which Part of the Constitution?

Explanation. Article 51A(g) is one of the Fundamental Duties listed in Part IVA of the Constitution.

Which constitutional amendment inserted both Article 48A and Article 51A(g)?

Explanation. Both provisions were inserted by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976.
