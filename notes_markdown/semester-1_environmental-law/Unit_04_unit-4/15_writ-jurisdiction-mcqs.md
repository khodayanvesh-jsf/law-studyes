## Meaning and Definition
Article 32 empowers which court to issue writs for enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

Explanation. Article 32 empowers the Supreme Court to issue writs for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

Article 226 gives every High Court writ power for:

Explanation. Article 226 covers both Fundamental Rights and any other legal right, making it wider in subject-matter than Article 32.

Which of the following correctly compares Article 226 with Article 32?

Explanation. Article 226 covers more subject-matter (Fundamental Rights + any other purpose) but is territorially confined to the High Court's jurisdiction, unlike Article 32's all-India reach.
