# 2. Right to wholesome environment
**Subject:** Environmental Law (Semester 1) | **Unit 4:** Unit 4
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/environmental-law/right-to-wholesome-environment/

## In One Minute
The Constitution does not use the words "right to a wholesome environment". The Supreme Court read the right into Article 21, which protects life and personal liberty. Because Article 21 is a Fundamental Right, a citizen can enforce it directly in the Supreme Court or a High Court. The right covers pollution-free water and air. It is not a licence to file any petition. The Court dismisses petitions that are really private grievances .

## Legal Provision
- Article 21: "No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law." Article 32: the right to move the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights. Article 226: the High Courts' power to issue writs to enforce Fundamental Rights "and for any other purpose". Supporting provisions: Article 48A (the State's directive on the environment) and Article 51A(g) (the citizen's duty).

## Meaning and Definition
The right to a wholesome environment is the right to live in a healthy, pollution-free surrounding. It is a judge-made right. Courts found it inside the word "life" in Article 21.

The Court has said that "life" means more than staying alive. In Subhash Kumar, it held that life includes the right to enjoy pollution-free water and air for the full enjoyment of life.

Can residents enforce the right? How to read this: start at the top. Each box must be answered "yes" to move down. The text beside a box shows where the claim fails. 1. Does the pollution affect, or is it likely to affect, the health of the people? Article 21 No: there is no breach of Article 21 to enforce. 2. Is the petition brought in genuine public interest? Subhash Kumar No: the court refuses relief. 3. Is the petition filed in the right court? Article 32 or Article 226 No: refile in the correct court or tribunal. 4. Result The court can order the pollution removed.

## Key Points to Remember
- The right is found in Article 21; the text does not mention it. Subhash Kumar (1991): life includes pollution-free water and air; petition dismissed. Enforced under Article 32 (Supreme Court) and Article 226 (High Courts). Rural Litigation Kendra (1985): a letter treated as an Article 32 petition. Noise beyond limits infringes Article 21 (In Re: Noise Pollution, 2005).
