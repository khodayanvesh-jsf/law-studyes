# 10. Role of Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/constitutional-law-i/role-of-drafting-committee-of-the-constituent-assembly/

## In One Minute
The Drafting Committee was a seven-member body set up by the Constituent Assembly on 29 August 1947. It examined the draft prepared by the Constitutional Adviser, B.N. Rau, and produced the Draft Constitution. Dr B.R. Ambedkar was its Chairman. Exams ask for the date, the members, the Chairman and the Committee's work. They also test the difference between the Adviser, the Committee and the Assembly.

## Legal Provision
- No Article of the Constitution deals with the Committee. These provisions record the Assembly's work.
- Preamble : the people, "in our Constituent Assembly" on 26 November 1949, adopted, enacted and gave themselves the Constitution. Article 394A: the Hindi translation, signed by members of the Constituent Assembly, is published under the President's authority.

## Meaning and Definition
The Drafting Committee was a committee of the Constituent Assembly . A body of nearly 300 members cannot write a legal text word by word. So the Assembly handed the task of putting its decisions into precise legal language to a small group of legal experts.

Constitutional Adviser Sir B.N. Rau prepared the first working draft. Drafting Committee Seven members under Dr Ambedkar examined and revised it. Constituent Assembly Debated the Draft clause by clause and adopted it. The Adviser's draft went to the Committee. The Committee's Draft Constitution went to the Assembly. The Committee did not adopt the Constitution. Only the Assembly could.

## Key Points to Remember
- Formed on 29 August 1947; seven members; Chairman Dr B.R. Ambedkar. Members: Gopalaswami Ayyangar, Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar, K.M. Munshi, Saadullah, Mitter, Khaitan. Krishnamachari (5 November 1948): one member resigned and was replaced, one died and was not replaced, one was away in America. Worked on B.N. Rau's draft; Draft Constitution sent in February 1948. Introduced in the Assembly on 4 November 1948; adopted on 26 November 1949. Ambedkar is the chief architect, an honorific title.
