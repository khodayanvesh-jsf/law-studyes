# 5. Indian Constitution
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester I) | **Unit 1:** Unit 1

## In One Minute
The Constitution of India is the written fundamental law of the country. The Constituent Assembly adopted it on 26 November 1949, and it came into force on 26 January 1950. It began with 395 Articles, 22 Parts and 8 Schedules. Amendments have since added Parts, letter-numbered Articles and four Schedules. Exams ask for these facts and the subject of each Part.

## Legal Provision
- Article 393: the short title is "the Constitution of India". Article 394: some Articles came into force at once, and the rest on 26 January 1950, called the "commencement of this Constitution". Article 394A: the President publishes an authoritative Hindi text, signed by members of the Constituent Assembly and updated for later amendments. Article 395: the Indian Independence Act, 1947 and the Government of India Act, 1935 are repealed.

## Meaning and Definition
The Indian Constitution is the single document that sets up the Union and the States, lists the rights of the people, and states how the country is governed. It opens with the Preamble , runs through Articles grouped into Parts, and ends with the Schedules.

## Key Points to Remember
- Adopted on 26 November 1949; in force from 26 January 1950. Original: 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules. Present: 25 Parts and 12 Schedules; new Articles carry letters such as 21A. Part III covers Fundamental Rights; Part IV Directive Principles; Part IVA Fundamental Duties. Part XX holds Article 368, the amending power. The Hindi text is authoritative under Article 394A.
