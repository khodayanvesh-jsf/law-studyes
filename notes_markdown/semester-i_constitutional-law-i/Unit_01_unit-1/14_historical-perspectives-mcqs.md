## Meaning and Definition
The Regulating Act, 1773 created the office of:

Explanation. It created the office of Governor-General of Bengal, first held by Warren Hastings, and set up a Supreme Court at Calcutta.

Pitt's India Act, 1784 is best known for introducing:

Explanation. The Company continued its commercial functions, while a new Board of Control took charge of political and administrative affairs.

Which Act first divided legislative and executive functions within the Governor-General's Council?

Explanation. The Charter Act, 1853 separated legislative and executive functions and introduced open competitive examinations for the Civil Service.
