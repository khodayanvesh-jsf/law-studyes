## Meaning and Definition
After the 44th Amendment Act, 1978, how many freedoms does Article 19(1) guarantee?

Explanation. The 44th Amendment, 1978 removed the right to property (clause (f)) entirely from Article 19(1), leaving six freedoms — speech/expression, assembly, association, movement, residence, and profession/trade/business.

Explanation.
