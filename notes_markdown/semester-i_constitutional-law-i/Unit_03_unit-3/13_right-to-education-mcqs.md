## Meaning and Definition
Article 21A, inserted by the 86th Constitutional Amendment (2002), makes free and compulsory education a fundamental right for children of what age group?

Explanation. Article 21A guarantees free and compulsory education to all children aged 6 to 14 years, in such manner as the State may, by law, determine.

Parliament gave effect to Article 21A by enacting which statute?

Explanation. The RTE Act, 2009 operationalises Article 21A, laying down norms for schools, teacher qualifications, prohibition of screening tests/capitation fees, and a 25% reservation for economically weaker/disadvantaged children in private unaided schools.

In Society for Un-aided Private Schools of Rajasthan v. Union of India (2012), the Supreme Court upheld the RTE Act's 25% reservation for economically weaker sections in:

Explanation. A three-Judge Bench upheld the 25 per cent rule as a reasonable restriction on the trade and profession of private schools. The majority exempted unaided minority schools, whose right comes from Article 30(1).
