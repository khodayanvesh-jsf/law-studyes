## Meaning and Definition
The Preamble to the Indian Constitution is derived from which document?

Explanation. The Preamble is drawn from the Objectives Resolution moved by Jawaharlal Nehru on 13 December 1946.

Which words were added to the Preamble by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976?

Explanation. The 42nd Amendment inserted "Socialist" and "Secular," and changed "unity of the Nation" to "unity and integrity of the Nation."

According to In Re: The Berubari Union (1960), what is the legal status of the Preamble?

Explanation. Berubari Union held the Preamble is not part of the Constitution and not directly enforceable, though it remains a key interpretive aid.
