## Meaning and Definition
Which Article guarantees the right to move the Supreme Court directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights?

Explanation. Article 32 guarantees the right to move the Supreme Court directly for enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

Unlike India, judicial review in the United States originated from:

Explanation. The U.S. Supreme Court asserted judicial review for itself in Marbury v. Madison (1803) — the American Constitution does not expressly grant it.
