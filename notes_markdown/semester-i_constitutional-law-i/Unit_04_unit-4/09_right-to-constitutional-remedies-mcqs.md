## Meaning and Definition
Who told the Constituent Assembly on 9 December 1948 that without Article 32 the Constitution would be a dead letter?

Explanation. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar told the Constituent Assembly on 9 December 1948 that without Article 32 the Constitution would be a dead letter.

Which court(s) have original jurisdiction to issue writs for enforcement of Fundamental Rights under Article 32?

Explanation. Article 32 vests the Supreme Court with original jurisdiction to issue directions or writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights.

Which writ is issued to secure the release of a person unlawfully detained?

Explanation. Habeas corpus ('to have the body') is issued to produce a detained person before the court and secure release if the detention is unlawful.
