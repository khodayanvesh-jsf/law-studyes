# 7. Fundamental Duties
**Subject:** Constitutional Law – I (Semester I) | **Unit 5:** Unit 5
**Source:** https://nyayasaral.com/subjects/constitutional-law-i/fundamental-duties/

## In One Minute
Part IVA of the Constitution has one Article, Article 51A. It lists eleven duties of every citizen, from respecting the Constitution to protecting the environment. The 42nd Amendment inserted it, and the 86th Amendment added clause (k). The Article itself creates no offence and no penalty. Exams ask you to list the duties, to say how they differ from Fundamental Rights, and whether they can be enforced. A common problem asks if a citizen can be prosecuted for breaking a duty.

## Legal Provision
- Article 51A: it shall be the duty of every citizen of India: (a) to abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions, the National Flag and the National Anthem; (b) to cherish and follow the noble ideals which inspired our national struggle for freedom; (c) to uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India; (d) to defend the country and render national service when called upon to do so; (e) to promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood amongst all the people of India, transcending religious, linguistic and regional or sectional diversities, and to renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women; (f) to value and preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture; (g) to protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers and wild life, and to have compassion for living creatures; (h) to develop the scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform; (i) to safeguard public property and to abjure violence; (j) to strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity, so that the nation constantly rises to higher levels of endeavour and achievement; (k) who is a parent or guardian, to provide opportunities for education to his child or ward between the age of six and fourteen years. Insertion: the 42nd Amendment inserted Part IVA and Article 51A with clauses (a) to (j), in force from 3 January 1977. The 86th Amendment inserted clause (k), in force from 1 April 2010.

## Meaning and Definition
A Fundamental Duty is an obligation that the Constitution names for each citizen. Part III tells citizens what they may claim from the State. Part IVA tells them what they owe the country. The text speaks of "every citizen", so the duties are owed by citizens, not by every person.

The Constitution does not say what happens if a citizen fails in a duty. Article 51A has no clause on punishment. A duty becomes an offence only if Parliament makes a law for it.

## Key Points to Remember
- Part IVA has one Article, Article 51A. The 42nd Amendment (in force 3 January 1977) added clauses (a) to (j). The 86th Amendment added clause (k), in force from 1 April 2010. The duties bind every citizen. Article 51A has no penalty. A law is needed to punish. Bijoe Emmanuel: no law obliges singing the Anthem. Clause (g) is paired with Article 48A (M.C. Mehta cases; Animal Welfare Board; Hanuman Laxman Aroskar). Clause (k) is paired with Article 21A.
