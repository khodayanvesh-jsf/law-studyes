In Golaknath v. State of Punjab (1967), the Supreme Court held that Parliament:

Explanation. By a 6:5 majority, the Court held Parliament cannot amend Part III so as to take away or abridge Fundamental Rights — a ruling later overruled in Kesavananda Bharati.

Which amendment directly reversed Golaknath by amending Articles 368 and 13?

Explanation. The 24th Amendment, 1971 restored Parliament's express power to amend any part of the Constitution, including Fundamental Rights.

Article 31C, as originally inserted by the 25th Amendment, protected laws implementing which provisions from Article 14/19 challenge?

Explanation. Article 31C originally protected only laws genuinely implementing Article 39(b) and 39(c).
