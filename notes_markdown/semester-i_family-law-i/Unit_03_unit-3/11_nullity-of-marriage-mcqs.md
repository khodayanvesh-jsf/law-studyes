## Meaning and Definition
A marriage is void under Section 11 if it contravenes conditions in Section 5 relating to:

Explanation. Section 11 makes a marriage void if it contravenes Sec. 5(i) bigamy, 5(iv) prohibited relationship, or 5(v) sapinda relationship.

Explanation. A void marriage never had legal existence from the very moment it was performed.

Which of the following is a ground for a voidable marriage under Section 12?

Explanation. Impotence preventing consummation is a Section 12 ground for a voidable marriage.

A voidable marriage remains valid:

Explanation. A voidable marriage is fully valid and subsisting until a competent court annuls it by decree.
