## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Hindu Marriage Act deals with judicial separation?

Explanation. Section 10 of the Hindu Marriage Act deals with judicial separation.

What is the primary legal effect of a judicial separation decree?

Explanation. Judicial separation releases the parties from the duty to cohabit, but the marriage continues to exist.
