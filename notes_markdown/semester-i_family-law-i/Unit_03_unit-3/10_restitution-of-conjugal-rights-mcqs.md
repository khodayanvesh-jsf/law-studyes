## Meaning and Definition
Which section of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 deals with restitution of conjugal rights?

Explanation. Section 9 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 provides the remedy of restitution of conjugal rights.

What must the petitioner prove to succeed under Section 9?

Explanation. The core requirement under Section 9 is withdrawal from society without reasonable excuse.

Once withdrawal from society is proved, on whom does the burden shift to prove a reasonable excuse?

Explanation. The burden shifts to the respondent once withdrawal from society is established.
