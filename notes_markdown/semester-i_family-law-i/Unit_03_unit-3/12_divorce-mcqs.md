## Meaning and Definition
Under the fault theory of divorce, the petitioner must prove:

Explanation. The fault theory requires proof of a specific matrimonial offence committed by the respondent.

Which of the following is NOT currently a ground for divorce under Section 13(1)?

Explanation. Section 13(1) now lists adultery, cruelty, desertion for two years, conversion, mental disorder, communicable venereal disease, renunciation of the world and presumed death. Clause (iv) of section 13(1) was omitted by Act 6 of 2019.

How many special grounds does section 13(2) give to a wife?

Explanation. Section 13(2) gives a wife four more grounds: pre-1955 polygamy of the husband, rape, sodomy or bestiality by him, non-cohabitation for a year after a maintenance decree, and repudiation of a marriage solemnized before she was fifteen.
