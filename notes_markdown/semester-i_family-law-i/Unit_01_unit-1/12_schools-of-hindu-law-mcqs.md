## Meaning and Definition
The Mitakshara school is based on a commentary by which author on the Yajnavalkya Smriti?

Explanation. The Mitakshara is a commentary by Vijnaneshwara on the Yajnavalkya Smriti.

According to Vineeta Sharma (para 20), the Mitakshara applies to most parts of India except:

Explanation. Para 20 of Vineeta Sharma records that the Mitakshara applies to most parts of India except Bengal.

Under the Mitakshara school, when does a son acquire a coparcenary interest in ancestral property?

Explanation. Under Mitakshara, a son (and now a daughter) acquires a coparcenary interest by birth, not on the father's death.
