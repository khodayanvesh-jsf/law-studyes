## Meaning and Definition
Under section 1(2), the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956 extends to:

Explanation. Section 1(2) says the Act extends to the whole of India.

Which section of the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956 applies the Act to Hindus, Buddhists, Jains and Sikhs?

Explanation. Section 2 applies the Act to Hindus in every form, Buddhists, Jains, Sikhs and the residuary group.

Which section of the Hindu Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956 gives it overriding effect over prior Hindu law and custom?

Explanation. Section 4 overrides any prior Hindu text, rule, custom, or inconsistent earlier law on matters this Act covers.
