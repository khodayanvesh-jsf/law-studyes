## Meaning and Definition
How many conditions does Section 5 of the Hindu Marriage Act prescribe for a valid marriage?

Explanation. Section 5 lists five conditions: monogamy, mental capacity, age, prohibited relationship, and sapinda relationship.

A marriage solemnised in breach of the monogamy condition under Section 5(i) is:

Explanation. Breach of Section 5(i) makes the marriage void under Section 11.

Which case held that a bigamous second marriage is void and the second "wife" cannot claim maintenance as a wife?

Explanation. Yamunabai Anantrao Adhav v. Anantrao Shivram Adhav held the bigamous marriage void, with no maintenance as a wife.
