## Meaning and Definition
Under classical Hindu law, marriage was primarily regarded as:

Explanation. In Tikait (1901) the Calcutta High Court said Hindu marriage is more religious than secular, and is a sacrament.

In Tikait Mon Mohini Jamadai v. Basanta Kumar Singh, the Calcutta High Court said the Hindu marital union is:

Explanation. The court said the union is indissoluble and a sacred tie (para 16).

In Tikait Mon Mohini Jamadai v. Basanta Kumar Singh, the court held that the deed making the husband live in his wife's family house:

Explanation. The court held that the rights and duties of the married parties are fixed by law and cannot be varied by their agreement. The deed was also against public policy.
